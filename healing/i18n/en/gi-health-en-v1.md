# GI Health Canon v1 (English) (2026-09-12)

> **Answering the owner's order**: "Cosmic-grade medicine · the full spectrum of human needs," lane 30 — gastrointestinal health. *Helicobacter pylori* (infecting about half the world's population · IARC Group 1 carcinogen), GERD, IBS, and constipation together trouble hundreds of millions of people — and "stomach nursing" (养胃, yǎng wèi) is among the most rumor-dense health topics in the Chinese-speaking world.
> **Honesty discipline**: hard numbers and key conclusions verified item by item to PMID title level / official pages (search channels = TinyFish + Europe PMC REST · built-in search not used · verification details in receipt); epistemic states never promoted unilaterally (labeled at each point in the text); this piece is an **evidence map + action cards**, not a diagnostic tool — any final judgment on "treat or not / scope or not" belongs to the gastroenterologist.

---

## 0. One-sentence master outline

**The right way to open GI problems = "red-flag triage + tiered action": functional problems without red flags (reflux / IBS / constipation) can mostly be managed with lifestyle and tiered medication, and "every test normal yet truly miserable" is a real disease (a functional GI disorder = disordered gut-brain regulation, not faking); but if any one of the six care-seeking red flags appears (progressive difficulty swallowing · black or bloody stools · weight loss + anemia · new "stomach" symptoms after 50 · pain that wakes you at night · an abdominal mass), rule out organic disease before any "nursing" talk — and H. pylori, an already-classified carcinogenic factor, deserves proactive testing, disciplined treatment, and post-treatment re-testing.**

Epistemic labeling: IARC Group 1 carcinogen, Rome IV and other official framings = FACT; disease mechanisms = MODEL; action cards = INFERENCE (evidence extrapolated to the personally actionable); "retained stool / gut toxins" etc. = CONTRADICTED; contested numbers honestly labeled CONTESTED / UNKNOWN.

## 1. The H. pylori face (Hp / H. pylori)

### 1.1 What it is, and where the evidence tier sits

- **H. pylori is a bacterium that colonizes the gastric mucosal epithelium**, discovered by Marshall and Warren in 1982 (a Nobel-grade discovery) — the old dogma "no bacteria can live in the stomach" was shattered, and the gastric ulcer went from "stress disease" to "infectious disease" (FACT · settled medical history).
- **IARC (the International Agency for Research on Cancer) lists "Helicobacter pylori (infection with)" as Group 1, a human carcinogen** (first evaluated 1994 · re-evaluated and maintained in Monograph 100B, 2012; IARC official List of Classifications directly checked: `Helicobacter pylori (infection with), 1, 61, 100B, 2012`) — this is the first-tier evidence grade for **gastric cancer**, not a vague food-scare concept (FACT).
- IARC 2026 work-report framing: **H. pylori infection is the leading cause of gastric cancer**; an IARC screen-and-treat program review published in NEJM 2025 confirms its place in population-level gastric-cancer prevention (FACT · official page / title level).
- **Global infection rate about one half** (Hooi 2017 · Gastroenterology systematic review PMID 28456631 · commonly cited framing 44.3% — this figure is an abstract-level quotation; the precise value defers to the original · INFERENCE). China is a region of relatively high infection burden, with marked household clustering (INFERENCE · five-consensus framing).

### 1.2 Test or treat — the tiered logic (action card · INFERENCE)

| Tier | Who is in this tier | Suggested direction |
|---|---|---|
| **Alarm symptoms** (see §6 red flags) | anyone | **gastroscopy first**, not breath test first — the endoscope looks at the mucosa directly and can take biopsies for Hp testing at the same time |
| **Symptomatic** (recurrent upper-abdominal pain / bloating / belching / acid reflux) | adults | test for Hp (urea breath test / stool antigen · accurate only after stopping PPIs and antibiotics), positive → disciplined eradication (the Kyoto consensus defines Hp gastritis as an infectious disease, Sugano 2015 · PMID 26187502 · FACT-grade consensus exists) |
| **Asymptomatic but family history** (first-degree relative with gastric cancer / Hp positive) | adults | proactive Hp screening + endoscopy assessed per local guidelines (household-transmission evidence + gastric-cancer primary-prevention benefit · INFERENCE) |
| **Asymptomatic, no family history, under 45** | low risk | the "test-and-treat" strategy applies to the **under-60 population without alarm symptoms** (Maastricht VI direction-level · Malfertheiner 2022 · PMID 35944925); from ≥40–45 start considering endoscopy per local gastric-cancer screening consensus (domestic consensus framing from age 40 · direction-level — local guidelines and your doctor prevail) |

### 1.3 How it is treated — the conceptual face of quadruple therapy (FACT-grade regimen exists · details belong to the doctor)

- Domestic standard first line: **bismuth quadruple therapy** (PPI acid suppressor + bismuth + two antibiotics), course **10–14 days** (Fifth National Consensus · Liu WZ 2018 · PMID 29512258; the Sixth Consensus added high-dose dual therapy as a first-line option; a Cochrane course review supports 14 days over 7 · Yuan 2013 · PMID 24338763).
- **Why combination therapy**: single-drug eradication rates are low + resistance is rising; quadruple = acid suppression boosts antibiotic potency + bismuth adds extra bactericidal action + dual antibiotics reduce resistance escape (MODEL · pharmacological mechanism).
- **Side effects, honestly**: bismuth black stools / black tongue (harmless · reversible), antibiotic diarrhea / taste disturbance; completing the full course matters more than stopping midway because "it feels better" — stopping halfway = breeding resistant bacteria (INFERENCE).
- **Eradication = primary prevention**: eradicating Hp in healthy asymptomatic carriers lowers gastric-cancer incidence (RCT systematic review · Ford 2014 BMJ · PMID 24846275; Cochrane 2020 update · PMID 32628791 — existing as FACT; effect size defers to the original · INFERENCE).

### 1.4 "Kissing spreads it / shared meals spread it" — the science, and the real meaning of separate servings

- The transmission-route evidence points to **person-to-person spread** (oral-oral / gastro-oral / fecal-oral, multiple routes); household clustering is a stable epidemiological fact (INFERENCE · Ahmed 2007 household hygiene and water-source study · PMID 17538754; the five consensuses explicitly recommend attention to household transmission · PMID 29512258).
- **Kissing transmission**: the theoretical route exists; evidence on the efficiency of single-kiss transmission between adults is limited — it is not an inevitable "kiss = disease" chain (INFERENCE · the population's main route = long-term shared-living exposure).
- **Shared meals and serving separation**: shared utensils / mouth-to-mouth feeding / pre-chewing food for children are the modifiable links in household clustering; separate servings + serving chopsticks + no pre-chewed food for children = low-cost exposure reduction (INFERENCE). **There is no need to panic or to distance yourself from family** — the correct response is "one person positive → the whole household gets tested as appropriate" (five-consensus framing, direction-level).
- Water and sanitation: safer drinking water and improved sanitation lower infection rates (INFERENCE · Ahmed 2007).

### 1.5 Post-treatment re-testing logic (action card · INFERENCE)

- **Re-testing after treatment is mandatory**: after completing eradication therapy, re-test **at least 4 weeks** later (urea breath test or stool antigen), and stop PPIs ≥2 weeks / antibiotics and bismuth ≥4 weeks before the re-test — otherwise false negatives (methodological FACT · consensus framing, direction-level).
- Why it is enforced: failure due to resistance is not rare; "no re-test = no way to know whether it worked," especially with a family history of gastric cancer / atrophic gastritis (INFERENCE).
- Positive re-test → switch regimen and re-treat (second-line regimen choice belongs to the doctor; WGO guideline framing includes 7–14-day bismuth quadruple among the directions · official PDF directly checked).

## 2. The GERD face

### 2.1 Mechanism: not "too much stomach acid," but "a loose gate + the wrong position"

- The core mechanism of reflux = **failure of the antireflux barrier**: transient relaxation of the lower esophageal sphincter (the cardia) (TLESR), hiatal hernia destroying the diaphragm-sphincter overlap, delayed gastric emptying raising intragastric pressure — **most people with reflux secrete a normal amount of acid** (MODEL · Mittal 1995 classic TLESR definition PMID 7615211; Kahrilas 2000 gastric-distension-induced TLESR and hiatal hernia · PMID 10734020).
- So "acid suppression" **reduces the burn** (symptomatic + heals esophagitis); it does not **repair the gate** (the cause) — this fixes the PPI's role and boundary (INFERENCE · extrapolated from mechanism).
- Aggravating factors (self-checkable): large meals, high-fat meals, eating lying down, late-night meals, coffee / alcohol, obesity (raised intra-abdominal pressure + more frequent TLESRs · Wu 2007 · PMID 17324403), smoking (INFERENCE-grade common knowledge).

### 2.2 PPIs used correctly, and the tiered answer to "is long-term use OK"

- PPIs (the omeprazole family) = first line for reflux esophagitis and symptom control (ACG GERD guideline · Katz 2022 · PMID 34807007 · FACT-grade guideline exists); **taking it 30–60 minutes before meals** is what matches the proton pumps' secretion rhythm (INFERENCE · pharmacokinetic framing).
- The tiered answer (INFERENCE · framework = AGA expert review Freedberg 2017 · PMID 28257716):
  - **Stop when you can**: after symptoms ease, use as-needed / intermittently; many patients do not need it every day.
  - **Take it when indicated**: severe erosive esophagitis, Barrett's, long-term NSAID gastroprotection and other clear indications — benefit > risk; long-term use ≠ error.
  - **Long-term use requires review**: those on long-term therapy periodically re-examine the indication and the lowest effective dose; the known associations (fracture / low magnesium / B12 / gut microbiome changes · Jackson 2016 · PMID 26719299) are mostly associational evidence; dose-response and reversibility after stopping are review items, not panic reasons (INFERENCE · mostly observational evidence, honestly labeled).
- The "dependence / addiction" claim is inaccurate: it is not addiction but rebound acid hypersecretion after stopping (physiological · resolves within weeks) — mistaking "rebound" for "can't quit" is a misjudgment (MODEL).

### 2.3 Position and timing interventions for night-time reflux (action card · INFERENCE)

- **Raise the head of the bed 10–15 cm** (raise the bed itself, not just the pillows — pillows alone fold the abdomen and add pressure); **no lying down within 3 hours after a meal**; ≥3 h between dinner and sleep; lying on the left side beats the right (the esophagus-stomach anatomy angle · INFERENCE).
- For night-dominant symptoms: split-dose PPI before breakfast and dinner, or adding a bedtime H2RA, belongs to the doctor's adjustment range — what you can do yourself is position + timing; both are zero-cost and mechanistically direct (MODEL→INFERENCE).

### 2.4 Barrett esophagus and the surveillance boundary of long-term severe reflux

- Long-term reflux is associated with Barrett esophagus and esophageal adenocarcinoma, but the **absolute risk is low** — a person with reflux should neither live in fear of cancer nor ignore it completely (Shaheen 2002 JAMA scientific review · PMID 11960540; Peters 2019 Nat Rev Dis Primers · PMID 31123267 · FACT-grade reviews exist).
- Surveillance boundary (direction-level · INFERENCE · GI guidelines and your doctor prevail): people with long-term / severe reflux may have one endoscopy; confirmed Barrett without dysplasia re-scopes at guideline intervals (years); dysplasia found → enter the radiofrequency-ablation treatment track — **one screening buys a defined follow-up curve, better than year after year of anxiety**.

### 2.5 "Baking soda water for heartburn" — instant relief and the rebound

- Baking soda (sodium bicarbonate) does chemically neutralize stomach acid — the instant relief is real (MODEL · acid-base neutralization).
- But three accounts must be settled: ① it produces CO2 gas → gastric distension → **may provoke TLESRs and trigger the next reflux instead** (MODEL · self-consistent with 2.1's mechanism); ② a sodium load (unfriendly to hypertension / edema); ③ repeated reliance masks persistent symptoms — **needing soda water every month to press down heartburn = time for Hp testing / endoscopy, not more soda** (INFERENCE).
- The "acid rebound" concept applies mainly to long-term heavy use of calcium-based antacids; soda water's long-term dependence face is mainly sodium load + symptom masking (CONTESTED boundary, honestly: mechanism-tiered · not the same evidence).

## 3. The IBS and functional GI disorder face

### 3.1 The gut-brain axis in plain words

- Gut and brain are connected by a **bidirectional neural-hormonal-immune highway** (enteric nervous system ≈ five hundred million neurons + the vagus nerve + microbiome-neuroimmune signaling) — stress in the brain can change gut motility / sensitivity, and gut signals can change mood and pain perception (MODEL · mechanism framework; interlink with the mood face: emotional problems and GI symptoms have high comorbidity — **treating the gut without settling the mind, or settling the mind without looking at the gut, both stall**).
- Interlink records: this section interlinks with the mood lane (the emotion-face canon); gut microbiome ecology is detailed in the microbiome volume (U08 · mb: anchor). Drugs such as PPIs change gut microbiome composition (Jackson 2016 · PMID 26719299 · FACT-grade experiments exist).

### 3.2 Rome IV at concept level (FACT-grade standard exists · details defer to the original)

- Rome IV (2016) formally renamed the functional GI disorders **"disorders of gut-brain interaction"** (Drossman 2016 · PMID 27144617; Lacy 2016 Bowel Disorders · PMID 27144627) — the renaming itself is a rehabilitation: **a dysregulation is a disease, not "thinking too much"** (FACT-grade naming change).
- IBS direction-level features: recurrent abdominal pain (≥1 day/week × 3 months) related to defecation / accompanied by changed bowel habit (constipation / diarrhea / mixed / unclassified type), without organic alarm evidence (FACT-grade standard exists).
- **The normalization narrative of "every test normal yet truly miserable"** (this canon's core humanistic face): normal endoscopy and blood tests ≠ "no disease"; it = "structure intact · regulation disordered" — visceral hypersensitivity (normal peristalsis perceived as pain), dysmotility, and microbiome-immune-neural axis dysregulation are all measurable, real mechanisms. The pain is real, not psychology; the treatment goal = **regulated back to controllable**, not "cured," and not "think positive" (INFERENCE · the normalization narrative is itself an evidence-based treatment element · a good doctor-patient relationship improves outcomes).

### 3.3 The low-FODMAP diet's evidence tier and execution boundaries

- Evidence tier: the low-FODMAP (fermentable oligo-/di-/monosaccharides + polyols) diet works for IBS symptoms — systematic review + network meta-analysis support (Black 2022 Gut · PMID 34376515 · FACT-grade review exists; effect sizes and evidence quality defer to the original · INFERENCE).
- **Execution boundaries (three hard boundaries · INFERENCE)**:
  1. **Three-phase system**: strict restriction phase (4–6 weeks) → systematic reintroduction (one food at a time, to find personal triggers) → individualized long-term version — **lifelong total restriction = incorrect use** (Whelan 2018 · PMID 29336079 · individualization framing);
  2. **Perfection not required**: do the cheaper moves first (regular meals / limiting alcohol and caffeine / less high-fat bingeing) before taking up FODMAP (INFERENCE · cost-effect ordering);
  3. **Caution in restriction-prone groups**: children / people with an eating-disorder history require professional supervision (INFERENCE).
- Non-drug comparison face: soluble fiber, antispasmodics, peppermint oil, and gut-brain neuromodulators all have controlled evidence in IBS (Ford 2008 BMJ · PMID 19008265; Black 2020 Lancet Gastroenterol Hepatol · PMID 31859183 · FACT-grade reviews exist) — **IBS is not a two-option menu of "endure" and "take probiotics"** (INFERENCE).

## 4. The constipation face

### 4.1 The Rome criteria in plain words (FACT-grade standard exists · Rome IV functional constipation B3)

Direction-level features: in ≥25% of defecations, two or more of — straining, lumpy hard stools (Bristol types 1–2), a sense of incomplete evacuation, an anorectal obstruction sensation, needing manual assistance; bowel movements <3/week; rarely loose stool without laxatives; and the IBS criteria not met. **"You must go once a day to be healthy" is a pseudo-standard — regular soft stools once every three days = normal** (INFERENCE · checked against the Rome criteria).

### 4.2 The real potency and onset timeline of dietary fiber + water

- Fiber improves chronic constipation with systematic-review support (Suares & Ford 2011 · PMID 21332763 · FACT-grade review exists) — **soluble fiber (e.g., psyllium / ispaghula) has the best evidence**; coarse insoluble fiber (large amounts of wheat-bran products) can worsen bloating in slow-transit constipation instead (INFERENCE).
- Onset timeline, honest version: **counted in days to weeks, not same-day magic**; fiber needs to be paired with ample water (MODEL · fiber absorbs water to form stool; INFERENCE · water is the precondition, not the drug).
- Order of operations: diet + water + movement + toileting habit first (morning on waking / after meals, riding the gastrocolic reflex · a squat position or a footstool to mimic squatting) — escalate to medication only if this layer is not enough (INFERENCE · stepwise logic).

### 4.3 Laxatives in three classes (FACT-grade pharmacological classes · usage details belong to the doctor / pharmacist)

| Class | Representatives | Mechanism | Long-term face (honest) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Bulk-forming** | psyllium / methylcellulose | absorbs water, adds bulk · softens stool | closest to physiology; usable long-term (with water · INFERENCE) |
| **Osmotic** | polyethylene glycol (PEG) / lactulose | holds water osmotically | good safety record · usable fairly long-term (under a doctor's guidance · INFERENCE) |
| **Stimulant** | senna / bisacodyl / rhubarb family | stimulates enteric nerves to move the bowel | **honestly**: needing one in order to defecate = time to find the cause; the old narrative that "stimulants degenerate the enteric nerves" has weak evidence, mainly from early animal studies (CONTESTED · not to be used to frighten people), but **dependence + tolerance + masking organic disease** is a real risk face (INFERENCE) — fine as short-term rescue; not viable as a long-term meal |

### 4.4 Falsifying "retained stool / detox" (CONTRADICTED)

- "Retained stool" (宿便) is **not a medical concept** — no textbook or guideline carries this diagnosis; the colonic mucosa is not a "toxin-storing wall," and colon contents are stool in continuous turnover anyway. "Clear retained stool = detoxify and beautify" is marketing copy for enemas / laxatives / "detox supplements" (CONTRADICTED · concept falsified).
- The real cost of long-term enemas / reliance on stimulant "detox teas" = electrolyte derangement (melanosis coli recorded in some cases · anthraquinone pigment deposition · INFERENCE) and delayed diagnosis of organic disease (INFERENCE).

### 4.5 When to have a colonoscopy (action card · INFERENCE · the final call belongs to the doctor)

- Constipation combined with **any red flag** (blood in stool / black stools, weight loss, anemia, abdominal pain that wakes at night, a mass, family history) → colonoscopy first; no "let's try nursing it."
- Constipation + age ≥ the screening start (§5) → complete a baseline colonoscopy along the way.
- Refractory constipation unresponsive to standard therapy → rule out structural / motility problems (slow transit / outlet obstruction · testing belongs to the doctor).

## 5. The GI cancer screening face

### 5.1 Endoscopy = the gold standard (direction-level · local guidelines and your doctor prevail)

- **Gastroscopy**: looks directly at the esophageal / gastric / duodenal mucosa and can biopsy — the gold standard of gastric-cancer screening; domestic consensus direction: those ≥40 with gastric-cancer high-incidence region / family history / Hp positive / past atrophic gastritis and similar factors enter screening (direction-level · local guidelines prevail).
- **Colonoscopy**: the gold standard of colorectal-cancer screening — it examines and treats in one pass (see 5.3).
- Starting ages, direction-level (**different by country · local guidelines prevail**): in the US, USPSTF in 2021 lowered the colorectal-cancer screening start for the general-risk population to **age 45** (official recommendation 2021-05 · multi-source directly checked); the domestic program framing: screening age band **40–74** for the sporadic high-risk population (National Health Commission 2024 screening program · official page directly checked; the 2023 Chinese expert consensus on early colorectal diagnosis and treatment uses the same 40–74). **Family history (first-degree relative with colorectal cancer / advanced polyp) → start earlier (generally = the relative's diagnosis age −10 years · direction-level) + more frequently** (INFERENCE · common guideline logic).

### 5.2 The stool occult blood + stool DNA alternative pathway

- An annual fecal immunochemical test (FIT), or stool DNA every 1–3 years (with or without FIT), is the non-invasive alternative pathway (among the USPSTF-listed screening options · direction-level); **a positive is not a death sentence — it is a dispatch order for colonoscopy** (INFERENCE · procedural logic).
- The price of non-invasive screening = the sensitivity / specificity trade-off and the colonoscopy burden after false positives — choose the pathway by accessibility + risk tier, discussed with your doctor (INFERENCE).

### 5.3 Polyp removal = the evidence for severing the cancer chain (the hardest card in this face)

- **Colonoscopic removal of adenomatous polyps cut colorectal-cancer incidence by about 76%** (National Polyp Study · Winawer 1993 NEJM · PMID 8247072 · FACT-grade study exists; the percentage is an abstract-level quotation · the original prevails).
- **23-year follow-up of the polypectomy cohort: colorectal-cancer mortality down about 53%** (Zauber 2012 NEJM · PMID 22356322 · FACT-grade study exists; percentage abstract-level quotation).
- Most colorectal cancers walk the "adenoma → carcinoma" multi-year slow path — **the value of colonoscopy is not "finding it"; it is "cutting out, on the spot, the thing not yet cancer"** (MODEL · the adenoma-carcinoma sequence).
- The fear that "scopes harm the body" is handled in §7; anesthesia concerns before the exam can be discussed with the doctor for a sedated ("painless") option (INFERENCE).

## 6. Red-flag chapter (any one → seek care promptly · every "nursing" suggestion in this piece yields immediately)

| # | Red flag | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| R1 | **Progressive difficulty swallowing** (food going down more and more blocked · solids to soft to liquids) | a progressive signal of esophageal stricture / tumor — "nurse it slowly" is not permitted |
| R2 | **Black stools / bloody stools / positive occult blood** | black stools = a common presentation of upper-GI bleeding; bloody stools require excluding colorectal organic disease; a positive occult blood = a colonoscopy dispatch order |
| R3 | **Unexplained weight loss + anemia** | a screening signal of malignancy / malabsorption and other systemic disease |
| R4 | **New "stomach disease" symptoms after 50** | new-onset dyspepsia in the screening age band = exclude organic disease first (new symptoms in a previously asymptomatic age = evidence of a different weight) |
| R5 | **Pain that wakes you at night** (pain that wakes you up · especially regular night-time pain) | an organic ulcer / tumor-type pattern — functional pain rarely wakes you at night |
| R6 | **An abdominal mass** | any palpable mass, seek care immediately |

- The correct action when a red flag appears: **book gastroenterology / emergency evaluation directly**, while self-checking Hp testing and endoscopy indications (INFERENCE).
- Interlink: first-aid scenarios see the first-aid canon; red-flag recognition also belongs to need: emergency recognition and red flags (human-needs map L2).

## 7. Falsification face (≥7 · sweeping the rumor-dense zone of Chinese "stomach nursing," item by item)

| # | Claim | Verdict | One-line truth |
|---|---|---|---|
| F1 | **"Stomach nursing = drinking congee"** (long-term white congee to nurse the stomach) | **CONTRADICTED** (as a long-term nursing method) | the stomach is a "use it or lose it" organ: long-term soft, mushy, liquid food lowers the digestive "load training" and functional adaptation declines; more crucial — **congee is a high-glycemic semi-liquid that empties fast, and people with reflux reflux more easily after drinking it** (MODEL · liquid gastric-emptying and positional-reflux mechanisms); a short-term liquid diet after surgery / in the acute phase is a doctor-ordered scenario, not daily stomach nursing |
| F2 | **"Bad stomach: eat less meat, drink more soup"** | **CONTRADICTED** (nutritional verdict) | soup's main components = water + purines / fat / salt + a little dissolved matter; **over 90% of the protein is in the meat, not the soup** (FACT-grade food-composition knowledge); "soup builds nutrition" is precisely the inefficient plan for patients who need protein (post-GI-surgery / chronic disease) — tender-stewed meat > soup |
| F3 | **"Retained stool / gut toxins"** | **CONTRADICTED** | "retained stool" is not a medical concept (§4.4); detox = a marketing word; electrolyte derangement and delayed diagnosis are its real outputs |
| F4 | **"Probiotics cure everything GI"** | **demoted · INFERENCE** (strain-specificity) | effects are **strain-specific + indication-specific**: individual strains have controlled evidence in specific scenarios (antibiotic-associated diarrhea / some IBS symptoms), but "one box of probiotics = the whole gut fixed" does not hold; the benefit evidence for long-term use in healthy people is thin (INFERENCE · honestly tiered); spend the money first on fiber + regular meals (proven gains), then consider specific strains (INFERENCE) |
| F5 | **"Chewing gum causes stomach disease / swallowed gum sticks in the gut for seven years"** | **CONTRADICTED** (urban legend) | normal gum chewing does not cause stomach disease (there are even records of it promoting salivation and postoperative bowel recovery); gum base is indigestible but also **does not get stuck** — it passes with the stool within days; exceptions for children swallowing large amounts (INFERENCE) |
| F6 | **"Gastroscopy / colonoscopy harm the stomach and body — skip them if at all possible"** | **CONTRADICTED** (the risk direction is reversed) | endoscopy complication rates are low (perforation / bleeding are rare events · direction-level); **the real cost of not scoping = polyps completing the adenoma-carcinoma sequence inside you** (§5.3's two NEJM cards); the fear itself is the quantifiable health cost (INFERENCE) |
| F7 | **"Stomach upset? Just skip a meal and it passes"** | **CONTRADICTED** (as a universal line for delaying red flags) | short-term functional discomfort can be self-limited; but applying "skip a meal" to the R1–R6 red flags = delaying organic diagnosis — **self-limited observation applies only to red-flag-free, mild, explainable scenarios, and observation has a time limit** (INFERENCE · red-line logic) |
| F8 | **"Stomach acid is bad; heartburn = too much acid, so suppress it hard"** | **demoted · MODEL correction** | acid is part of digestion and the barrier; reflux's main cause = barrier failure (§2.1) — acid suppression is a symptomatic tool, not "detox"; long-term heavy suppression needs indication review (§2.2 tiering) |
| F9 | **"A bowel movement once every three days = constipation; you must detox daily"** | **CONTRADICTED** (pseudo-standard) | Rome IV functional constipation reads form / straining / incomplete evacuation, not "daily check-ins"; regular soft stools = healthy, whether once in 3 days or twice a day (§4.1) |

## 8. Honest boundaries of this piece (UNKNOWN is a first-class citizen)

1. **Precise infection rates**: the global 44.3% (Hooi 2017) is an abstract-level quotation; China's province-by-province / age-band baselines = UNKNOWN (not verified item by item; no numbers invented).
2. **Screening ages differ by country and guideline and keep evolving**: this piece gives direction-level only (US 45 · domestic high-risk 40–74); **current local guidelines + your doctor prevail**.
3. **Effect sizes**: Hp eradication → gastric cancer (the Ford series), FODMAP effect sizes, polypectomy 76% / 53% are all review / abstract-level quotations; precise CIs defer to the originals.
4. **The stimulant-laxative "nerve damage" narrative**: the modern evidence face = CONTESTED (old animal evidence weak · the dependence and masking risks real) — this piece writes both halves honestly and takes no extreme side.
5. Not covered: peptic-ulcer bleeding management (first-aid territory · interlink with the first-aid canon), inflammatory bowel disease (IBD · a separate face pending v2), pediatric GI disease, and liver / biliary-pancreatic faces (pending their lanes · the original anat interlink anchor repaired 2026-09-13 · anatomy volume not yet built · original ids in receipt).
