# Depression & Anxiety Healing Canon — English L1 (2026-09-13)

> For anyone in a low place, and anyone beside them. **Face 5 (suicide intervention) is numbered fifth, not ranked fifth — highest priority; start there on any doubt.**

## Face 1 · Low mood ≠ depression

A disease needs all three [MODEL]: **≥2 weeks** of low mood/lost interest most days; **symptom cluster** (sleep, appetite, energy, concentration, guilt, death thoughts); **functional impact**. WHO: ~5.7% of adults; treatment works at every severity (FACT). **Somatization (躯体化)**: body-only complaints — "nothing found" is itself a clue.

## Face 2 · What works

Exercise (FACT, BMJ 2024, 178 RCTs): walking/jogging, yoga, strength — therapy/SSRI magnitude, "confidence still low", **"alongside", not instead**. CBT (认知行为治疗) (FACT): catch–check–replace; activate first. **Light therapy** (光照疗法) (FACT+MODEL): seasonal affective disorder (SAD, the depression subtype most prevalent in autumn–winter) has dedicated evidence for **morning bright-light exposure** (10000 lux light box · used on waking · usually 20–45 min); the Cochrane review team (May et al., abstract PMID 32111489) notes the evidence for **prevention** use remains limited — treatment and prevention are two different sentences. Mechanism sits on the light–circadian–mood pathway (systematically mapped in a 2026 Life review, PMID 41900968). People with ordinary depression need not buy a light box as a universal lamp; those with a clear seasonal pattern deserve a discussion with their doctor. Sleep deprivation can trigger mania — **never self-try** (FACT+MODEL).

## Face 3 · Medication, honestly

Antidepressants need **2–6 weeks** — side effects first; don't quit in week two; stopping is the prescriber's call [MODEL+FACT]. **Discontinuation syndrome (停药综合征) ≠ addiction** — no craving; taper slowly (FACT+MODEL). "Meds change personality" — falsified; untreated illness erodes personhood. **Seeing a doctor = normal**: psychiatric and psychological outpatient clinics are ordinary clinics — booking one is no different from going for hypertension or gastritis. Paths in the Chinese context: psychology/psychiatry departments of general hospitals, specialized mental-health centers, community-health referral — **seeking care for depression/anxiety is health care, not "being labeled"**. Records are private; a care-seeking record does not touch your personhood — it serves your health.

## Face 4 · Anxiety

Pathological when mismatched, unstoppable, avoidance shrinks life [MODEL]. Panic: adrenaline storm, **no cardiac danger**, peaks in minutes — the dying feeling is a symptom, not prophecy. GAD (广泛性焦虑) = unstoppable "what if"; social = fear of scrutiny. **Every avoidance tops up the next attack** — graded exposure is the way out (MODEL).

## Face 5 · Suicide intervention (highest priority — full)

> **Thoughts of ending your life right now? Reach a crisis line now — mainland China: 12356 (national hotline, 24 h, free), or the nearest emergency department.** Elsewhere: your local emergency number or findahelpline.com (US 988; UK/Ireland Samaritans 116 123). *(Translator calibration; source anchor = 12356.)* **Thoughts are symptoms, not destiny — one more night with treatment and companionship can change them.**

**5.1 Asking does not plant the idea (FACT)**: Dazzi 2014 (systematic review): asking about suicidal ideation neither increases nor induces suicidal thoughts or behavior; adolescents concur (PMID 21061872); being asked feels understood — it **opens** the door. Ask calmly, plainly: "Have things been so heavy you've had thoughts of not wanting to live?" · "Have you thought about hurting yourself?" Listen without judgment; probing plan/means/timing belongs to professionals — step one: ask, stay, help them reach care.

**5.2 Warning signs (MODEL/consensus)** — Verbal: farewells ("you'd be better off without me"), affairs in order, talk of dying, sudden "relief". Behavioral: treasured things given away, abrupt withdrawal, researching means, farewell writing, abandoning treatment. State: severe depression, drastic insomnia change, surging substance use, major loss or humiliation. **Stacked signals = act today.**

**5.3 Calm ≠ safe (MODEL)**: "they seemed peaceful / they smiled = danger passed" is a dangerous misread — the decided may feel sudden calm because "the pain ends soon"; the struggle may have ended **in their mind**. **Calm never stands down the alert.** Danger peaks in the **first weeks of "getting better"** — mood not restored, capacity back.

**5.4 Lines**: 12356 [FACT: NHC document 2024-12-25, nationwide 2025-05-01, free, 24 h]. **Actively self-harming, ingested something, or concrete plan without safety → 120 or straight to the ED — don't phone around first.** Psychiatric ED/crisis clinics; students add school counseling.

**5.5 Companionship (MODEL)**: ① present, unjudging — "thank you for telling me, I'm here"; ② no debates, no sermons; ③ push professional help and **remove means** (medication, sharps — evidence-based). Strongest sentence: **"I've caught this thought with you — we'll find a professional, and I'm coming with you."**

## Face 6 · Stigma

Stigma measurably delays help-seeking (Clement 2015, 144 studies — FACT). "Being dramatic (矫情)" redefines illness as character defect → self-stigma → silence → worse course (MODEL). Family culture (家文化) double-edged: protective (first care team) and harmful (**face (面子)** events, overridden decisions) — decision stays with patient and clinician (MODEL).

## Face 7 · Red flags (preserved in full)

**Green (schedulable)**: low mood/lost interest **>2 weeks** with functional impact (all three criteria); unrevealing workups + gray mood; insomnia/early waking >2 weeks; numbness toward once-loved things.
**Yellow (weeks)**: panic several per week or new avoidance; worry dominating most days uncontrollably; sleep/appetite worsening; 2–4 weeks self-help without trend.
**Red (today, now)**: **any thought of self-harm or suicide** (even a fleeting flash deserves to be caught); self-harm acted on, ingestion, plan made; command hallucinations or persecution beliefs (urgent — may not be unipolar); others report affairs in order or treasures given away.
**Dialects (MODEL)** — Adolescents: irritability replaces low mood; grade crashes, school refusal, wiped/farewell accounts, somatic sick leave, hidden scratches; asking does not induce (PMID 21061872) — ask gently. Elders: somatization + cognitive change mislabeled "just aging"; late-life risk underestimated, older men highest; "suddenly quiet, sorting affairs" — take seriously; bring the medication list.

## Face 8 · Myths falsified

1. "Think positive" — implies the pain is your inadequacy.
2. "Depression = dramatic/weak" — stigma discourse, not medicine.
3. "Exercise cures all" — **one of** the core treatments, never a substitute when severe/suicidal.
4. "Meds forever" — typically ≥4–9 months post-remission; taper slowly.
5. "Therapy is paid chatting" — structured evidence-based treatment.
6. "Asking induces suicide" — evidence says no; **not asking is the real risk**.

## Closing

If in doubt, go. Triad: presence, listening, push toward care. *(Honesty clause: mechanisms open; panic windows are conventions; dosing belongs to face-to-face care.)*
