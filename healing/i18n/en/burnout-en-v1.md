## 0. One-line collapse

**Burnout is not a character flaw; it is an exposure response to "chronic occupational stress that has not been successfully managed" — the exposure face (working hours / night shifts / low control) carries hard disease-consequence evidence; on the intervention face the organizational layer's evidence is stronger than the individual layer's; no drug has an approved indication; in China's overtime context what an individual can do is four things: "boundaries + recovery + sleep + red-flag recognition."**

Epistemic labeling: definitions and exposure-consequence associations = FACT grade (WHO official documents + title-level-verified metas); mechanism chains = MODEL; action cards = INFERENCE (extrapolated from evidence to personally operable steps); rumors such as "burnout is a disease and needs medication" = CONTRADICTED.

## 1. The burnout-science face

### 1.1 The WHO ICD-11 definition (verbatim-verified · FACT)

ICD-11 lists burn-out (code **QD85**) as an **occupational phenomenon, not classified as a medical condition**, placed in the chapter of "factors influencing health status or contact with health services." WHO's official definition (announcement of 2019-05-28 · verbatim):

> "Burn-out is a syndrome conceptualized as resulting from chronic workplace stress that has not been successfully managed. It is characterized by three dimensions:
> ① feelings of energy depletion or exhaustion;
> ② increased mental distance from one's job, or feelings of negativism or cynicism related to one's job;
> ③ reduced professional efficacy.
> Burn-out refers specifically to phenomena in the occupational context and should not be applied to describe experiences in other areas of life."

Three honest points:
- **"Occupational phenomenon, not disease" is WHO's explicit ruling** (FACT) — burnout's intervention target is the **workplace exposure**, not "treating the patient";
- The definition is bounded to the occupational context — household burnout / academic burnout / childcare burnout are **analogical uses**, outside the ICD-11 definition's domain (FACT · definition boundary);
- ICD-10 already carried an entry in the same chapter; ICD-11 gives the three-dimensional detailed definition for the first time (FACT · WHO announcement original text).

### 1.2 The measurement face (MODEL · a scale is operationalization, not truth)

| Instrument | Content | Epistemic state and anchor |
|---|---|---|
| **MBI (Maslach Burnout Inventory)** | Three dimensions aligned with ICD-11: emotional exhaustion / depersonalization (or cynicism) / personal accomplishment (inverse) | The de-facto dominant scale — 85.7% (156/182) of studies in the physician-prevalence systematic review used the MBI (FACT·Rotenstein JAMA 2018, PMID 30326495) |
| **CBI (Copenhagen Burnout Inventory)** | Asks the degree of "burnout" itself: personal burnout / work burnout / burnout toward those one serves | Proposed by Kristensen 2005 (Work & Stress · original journal not in Medline, MODEL-grade provenance); the Chinese (Taiwan) version's workplace psychometric validation (FACT·Int J Behav Med 2007, PMID 18062055); a physician CBI meta already exists (BMC Health Serv Res, PMID 42277822 · title-level) |
| UWES (engagement scale) | Measures work engagement, burnout's opposite pole | MODEL · anchor not independently verified this round (UNKNOWN · not expanded) |

### 1.3 The framing trap (this canon's most important measurement warning · FACT)

**Rotenstein et al. JAMA 2018 (PMID 30326495)**: 182 studies, 109,628 individuals, 45 countries — **142 independent definitions of "overall physician burnout" coexisted**, with ≥47 "overall burnout" criteria within the MBI family alone; **reported overall-burnout prevalence ranged from 0% to 80.5%**. The authors had planned a meta-analytic pooling but were forced by heterogeneity to abandon pooling and publish a descriptive review.

**Inference (INFERENCE · directly usable)**: any single-value citation of "industry X's burnout rate is Y%" is almost certainly a framing artifact. The correct reading:
1. First ask which scale + which criterion was used (all three dimensions high? exhaustion only? a single self-rated item?);
2. Make only **same-instrument, same-criterion longitudinal/horizontal comparisons**; absolute values across studies are not comparable;
3. Down-weight media headlines of the "global burnout rate 90%" type directly.

### 1.4 Prevalence ranges (anchored values with framing · FACT)

- **Pooled prevalence of burnout symptoms in nurses worldwide, 11.23%** (systematic review of 113 studies + meta of 61 studies · 45,539 nurses · 49 countries; marked regional/specialty/scale variation — highest in sub-Saharan Africa, lowest in Europe; Woody et al. J Psychiatr Res 2020, PMID 32007680). Note this is a **pooled same-criterion value within a single meta**, not in contradiction with other reviews' "30–50%" — the criteria differ.
- **Physicians**: see 1.3 — 0%–80.5%, not poolable (FACT).
- **General working population**: because framing depends on scale and criterion, this canon **cites no single value** (honest boundary · UNKNOWN: a same-criterion meta on a representative sample of China's full-time workers was not retrieved this round — left as a gap).

## 2. Hard evidence on health consequences (why occupational health is an L2 safety-layer question)

### 2.1 Long working hours × cardiovascular death — the WHO×ILO 2021 verdict (FACT · dual anchors verified verbatim)

**Anchor one (the paper)**: Pega F, Náfrádi B, Momen NC, et al. *Global, regional, and national burdens of ischemic heart disease and stroke attributable to exposure to long working hours for 194 countries, 2000-2016: A systematic analysis from the WHO/ILO Joint Estimates of the Work-related Burden of Disease and Injury.* **Environ Int 2021;154:106595** (PMID 34011457 · abstract numbers verified verbatim):
- In 2016, **488 million people** (472–503 million) worldwide were exposed to long working hours (**≥55 hours/week**), 8.9% of the global population;
- Attributable deaths **745,194** (705,786–784,601) · attributable **23.3 million DALYs**;
- Population attributable fractions (PAF): ischemic heart disease deaths 3.7%, **stroke 6.9%** — long working hours are the **largest single occupational disease-burden risk factor globally** (about one third of the occupational disease burden · WHO official framing).

**Anchor two (the official press release)**: WHO/ILO joint announcement of 2021-05-17 (verified verbatim):
- 745,000 deaths = **stroke 398,000 + heart disease 347,000**; a 29% increase over 2000 (heart-disease attributable deaths +42%, stroke +19%);
- **72% of the deaths were male**; deaths occurred mostly at ages 60–79 — people who had worked ≥55 hours/week during ages 45–74 (years of exposure → late-life outcome · dose logic);
- **Relative risks (vs 35–40 hours/week)**: ≥55h/week → **stroke risk +35%**, **ischemic heart disease death risk +17%**.

**The evidence chain upstream and downstream (same WHO/ILO series · title-level verified)**:
- Long hours → ischemic heart disease meta: Li J, Pega F, et al. Environ Int 2020;142:105739 (title-level);
- Long hours → **depression** meta: Rugulies R, Pega F, et al. Environ Int 2021;155:106629 (title-level · the WHO/ILO series' first inclusion of psychological outcomes in the joint estimates);
- Long hours → alcohol use meta: Pachito DV, Pega F, et al. Environ Int 2021;146:106205 (title-level).

> **Honest boundary**: the attributable fractions rest on observational evidence + modeling (exposure data from 2,324 cross-sectional surveys); "causes" is WHO's formal word under its sufficient-evidence standard, but at the individual level "55 hours a week → I will definitely have a heart attack" does not hold (population attribution ≠ individual prophecy · INFERENCE).

### 2.2 Night shifts and the carcinogen list (FACT · title-level verified)

- **In 2019 IARC classified "night shift work" as Group 2A (probably carcinogenic to humans)**. Anchor: the IARC Monographs Vol 124 working-group paper *Carcinogenicity of night shift work.* **Lancet Oncol 2019;20:1058-1059** (PMID 31281097 · title-level verified). The 2007 first evaluation concerned "shift-work involving circadian disruption"; the 2019 update shifted to "night shift work" and **maintained 2A** (secondary sources consistent: IARC publications page · ISGlobal 2019-07-15).
- 2A = "probably carcinogenic to humans" — **neither** "definitely carcinogenic" (Group 1) **nor** "no risk" (honest · graded semantics FACT). Mechanism candidate = circadian disruption → melatonin suppression (see the 2.3 chain).
- Night-shift–breast-cancer observational metas: Carcinogenesis 2021 (PMID 34409980 · title-level) + a two-stage dose-response meta in BMC Public Health 2024 (PMID 39085800 · title-level).
- Night-shift–cardiovascular dose-response meta: Front Public Health 2025 (PMID 41069800 · title-level); night-shift–type-2-diabetes cohort meta: BMC Endocr Disord 2024 (PMID 39696306 · title-level).
- **UNKNOWN, stated honestly**: the 2A classification addresses "night shift work" as a whole; for the individual question "after N years of night shifts, will I get cancer," no individualized probability can be given — a classification is hazard identification, not an individual risk function.

### 2.3 Occupational chronic stress → physiological chain (MODEL · wired to the existing PNI pack)

The chain (each link has evidence of its own; the whole chain is a model, not a single law):
**chronic occupational stress → HPA-axis/cortisol-rhythm disruption + sympathetic activation → (sleep destruction · immune dysregulation · worsening blood pressure/lipids/glucose metabolism) → cardiovascular events / depression / infection susceptibility**.
- Cortisol face: systematic review of peripheral cortisol in burnout and major depression (Neurosci Biobehav Rev 2020, PMID 32088345 · title-level) — the direction of cortisol change in burnout is inconsistent across studies (**honest: not a simple "burnout = cortisol off the charts" story**); systematic review of melatonin/cortisol suppression and rhythm disruption in burnout among healthcare practitioners (Clin Pract 2025, PMID 41294630 · title-level).
- Immune/inflammation face: this repo's `pni-weave-pack-v1.json` already weaves the "chronic social stress → allostatic load → IL-6/CRP → CVD/depression" chain (same mechanism family reused · no duplicate anchor erected).
- Sleep face: wired to this repo's sleep canon — `slp:SLEEP-A1` (the sleep-pressure × circadian two-process model) and `mb:B1` (sleep factors).

## 3. Intervention evidence tiering (tiered by RCT quality · key section)

### 3.1 The tiering master table

| Tier | Intervention | Evidence strength | Anchors and honest boundaries |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Organizational/structural face** | Work-hour restructuring · schedule redesign · increasing job control · load redistribution | **Strongest** (randomization is less feasible, but effect sizes are largest and strike the exposure source directly) | Panagioti et al. Lancet (PMID 27692469 · abstract verified): structural/organizational and individual interventions **both** reduce burnout to clinically meaningful degrees; the JDC model's 30-year evidence face: job strain → coronary heart disease HR 1.23 (Kivimäki Lancet 2012, PMID 22981903 · IPD meta n=197,473) |
| **Individual face** | CBT-family · mindfulness/stress-reduction · coaching · peer support | **Moderate and decaying** (can move scores, hard to change exposure) | Same Panagioti: 15 RCTs (716 participants) + 37 cohorts (2,914 participants) — overall burnout 54%→44%, high exhaustion 38%→24%; a psychological-detachment intervention meta exists (J Occup Health Psychol 2021, PMID 34096763 · title-level) |
| **Medication** | No drug approved for a burnout indication | **Zero indications** | Fact layer: burnout is an occupational phenomenon, not a disease (ICD-11) → no registered indication can exist (INFERENCE extrapolated from definition + regulatory fact); "energizing supplements/stimulants" have no burnout RCT evidence, and stimulants worsen sleep destruction (MODEL · wired to mb:B1) |

### 3.2 Why the organizational face's evidence is strongest while the individual's is hard to change (honesty section)

- **The job demand-control model** (Karasek 1979 · MODEL): high demands × low control = high strain — the strongest link of the 30-year evidence face is **the association of job strain with coronary heart disease**: IPD pooling of 13 European cohorts, n=197,473, 1.49 million person-years, 2,358 events, **HR 1.23 (95%CI 1.10-1.37)**; PAR 3.4% (Kivimäki Lancet 2012).
- **Publication bias, stated honestly**: published studies HR 1.43 vs unpublished 1.16 — the true effect is close to 1.16–1.23 (FACT · disclosed in the original text). Kivimäki's own conclusion is equally honest: **the disease benefit of workplace-stress interventions is smaller than that of traditional risk factors such as smoking cessation** — no exaggeration.
- **The individual's bind, stated honestly**: low control is itself part of burnout exposure — asking the "low-control worker" to "increase their sense of control" contains a circular difficulty (INFERENCE). So the individual layer's rational target is the **controllable subset**: small boundaries within working hours, recovery experiences, sleep, red-flag recognition — not "changing your boss."

### 3.3 The individual face's effects and their decay (as they are)

- Mindfulness/CBT-family interventions on burnout scores are **effective but moderate in magnitude** (Panagioti: EE score −2.65 points · high-exhaustion share −14 percentage points), and **the longer the follow-up, the more visible the decay** is a pervasive finding in this field (MODEL · consistent direction across reviews; no single decay meta locked this round — UNKNOWN, stated honestly);
- **Psychological detachment (stopping work rumination after hours)** is the core construct of recovery-experience research (the Sonnentag school · MODEL); an intervention meta exists (PMID 34096763 · title-level), direction = trainable, with small-to-medium effects on well-being;
- Vacations: short-term effective but **fast-decaying** — systematic reviews and meta-analyses alike report vacation effects returning to baseline within weeks (de Bloom et al. J Occup Health 2009, PMID 19096200; J Appl Psychol 2025 meta, PMID 39836131 · both title-level; the decay-speed numbers are MODEL-grade · see §5 rumor 4).

## 4. China-context action cards (INFERENCE: extrapolated from the evidence above · not diagnosis)

### 4.1 First, three epistemic boundary lines

1. **Legal face**: articles 36/41 of the Labor Law = an 8-hour day, 44-hour week baseline + overtime caps of ≤3 hours/day and ≤36 hours/month (legal-text fact layer · cited by this canon as institutional text); between the WHO ≥55h/week risk line and the statutory 36h/month overtime cap there is no safety belt — **the WHO risk line itself is an exposure stratum of observational epidemiology, not a safety threshold** (INFERENCE).
2. **Individual ≠ population**: the 745,194 deaths are a population attribution; the action cards target **reducing exposure + improving recognition**, not manufacturing anxiety.
3. **Organizational first**: where schedules/hours can be negotiated (management roles / unions / consultation channels), go through the organizational face first — the strongest-evidence layer.

### 4.2 The individual's doable list (each item carrying its evidence source)

**A. Boundary setting (against the exposure face)**
- A1 If you can avoid signing the "voluntary overtime confirmation form," don't sign it; keep your own hours records (clock-ins/emails/IM timestamps) — in a dispute this is the only hard evidence (INFERENCE · rights-protection operability);
- A2 Self-check **total weekly hours** once a week: ≥55h/week = inside the WHO risk stratum (FACT·Pega); four consecutive weeks over the line = trigger the §4.3 self-check;
- A3 Micro-boundaries: mute non-core-hour messages; for the first 30 minutes after work, don't touch work messages (operationalized psychological detachment · MODEL);
- A4 In negotiable scenarios, replace "agreeing on the spot" with "a handover document + a schedule" — turning "sense of control" from a verbal feeling into a calendar fact (INFERENCE · JDC logic).

**B. Recovery experience (against the recovery face)**
- B1 After work, schedule one **specific activity unrelated to the work identity** (exercise / cooking / picking up the kids / walking the dog) — the key to detachment is "having something to engage with," not "lying down scrolling the phone" (MODEL · recovery-experience research: mastery experiences / low-effort activities beat passive leisure);
- B2 The vacation's positioning = a recovery "interval," not a "treatment": effects decay within weeks; the long-term solution remains hours and boundaries (FACT+MODEL · §3.3);
- B3 At least one complete "no-work" half-day block per weekend — fragmented rest ≠ recovery (MODEL · consistent direction of the recovery literature).

**C. Sleep protection (against the physiological chain · wired to mb:B1/slp cluster)**
- C1 A fixed wake time takes priority over a fixed bedtime (wired to the sleep canon's two-process model · slp:SLEEP-A1);
- C2 For insomnia during a burnout period, first line is still CBT-I, not self-purchased sleeping pills (FACT · this repo's sleep-textbook SLEEP-B1: 20 RCTs pooled · sleep-onset −19 minutes / wake-after-onset −26 minutes / efficiency +9.91%);
- C3 Night-shift workers: after the night shift, catch up on sleep in a dark environment + avoid "toughing it out" through the day after the shift — the goal is not eliminating circadian disruption (it cannot be eliminated) but **not stacking on top of it** (no caffeine after 2 p.m. · MODEL).

**D. Social connection (against the L3 belonging face)**
- D1 Once a week, meet someone "outside the work circle" face to face — social connection is a protective factor already woven in this repo (pni-weave-pack: social support → buffers stress → health chain);
- D2 Find 1–2 industry peers with whom you can speak truth: cynicism's (dimension ②) most effective early warning is "starting to mock the people one serves" — best caught early by others (INFERENCE).

### 4.3 Red flags for seeking care (listed separately · this part always defers to the doctor)

> **Master rule: burnout is not a disease, but it often travels with disease and is often disease's upstream disguise. Any one of the following → seek care; stop "self-regulating."**

| Red flag | Concrete line | Where to go |
|---|---|---|
| **Depression screening line** | Low mood / loss of interest **nearly every day, ≥2 weeks** + functional impairment (can't work / not eating / social shutdown) → depression screening (PHQ-9 self-screen ≥10 is the moderate line · triage use only) | Psychiatry/psychology outpatient |
| **Persistent somatization** | Chest tightness / palpitations / headache / GI upset **persisting ≥2 weeks and decoupled from the hours curve** (rest doesn't relieve it) → rule out organic disease first | Internal medicine/general practice first |
| **Cardiovascular acute line** | Chest pain at rest / radiating pain / cold sweat / shortness of breath → **emergency immediately** (long-hours-exposed people are already a population with elevated IHD/stroke risk · Pega +17%/+35%) | Emergency department |
| **Sleep collapse** | ≥3 consecutive weeks of sleep-onset difficulty or early waking + impaired daytime function → insomnia specialty/CBT-I (not self-purchased sleeping pills) | Sleep clinic/psychiatry |
| **Substance dependence** | Needing alcohol/stimulants/sleeping pills to get off work or fall asleep → addiction-medicine evaluation | Psychiatry/addiction clinic |
| **Self-harm ideation** | Thoughts of "not wanting to live" → **immediately** psychological assistance hotline / emergency department | Crisis intervention |

### 4.4 What is not an action card (the negative list)

- No endorsement of self-purchased "anti-fatigue supplements/energizing miracle pills" (§3.1 medication row + §5 rumor 5);
- No endorsement of "quit-cold-turkey solutions" (§5 rumor 3);
- This canon is not a diagnostic tool: QD85 is a "health-status factor" code, not a diagnostic code — **diagnosis and sick-leave certificates belong to the clinician**.

## 5. The verification/falsification face (five internet rumors, one by one)

| # | Rumor | Verdict | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Burnout = laziness / being dramatic / not trying hard enough" | **CONTRADICTED** | The ICD-11 definition = a syndrome of chronic occupational stress not successfully managed (FACT·§1.1); 488 million people exposed and 745,194 attributable deaths are exposure-driven, not character-driven (FACT·§2.1); "laziness" cannot explain the dose-response (the longer the hours, the higher the risk). Burnout's common reverse presentation = a history of long-term high engagement (MODEL · the three dimensions' developmental history). |
| 2 | "Burnout is just depression — take antidepressants" | **CONTRADICTED (as an equivalence claim)** | Related but not identical: burnout-depression r=.52, burnout-anxiety r=.46, meta conclusion "no conclusive overlap…different and robust constructs" (FACT·Koutsimani PMID 30918490); ICD-11 explicitly not a medical condition → no drug indication (FACT+INFERENCE·§3.1); the cortisol profile does not match MDD's (PMID 32088345). **But**: burnout's comorbid depression risk is real → seek care when care is due (§4.3); both things hold at once. |
| 3 | "Change jobs and the burnout is solved" | **Partly holds · partly CONTRADICTED (Model/INFERENCE)** | The holding side: burnout is an exposure response → moving to a "low-demand × high-control" job genuinely removes the exposure source (the JDC logic · the reverse face of HR 1.23). The non-holding side: ① if the new job's hours/control don't change, the symptoms travel with you; ② under economic constraints, "just quit" is a survivor's narrative; ③ the vacation effect's brevity works the same way — environment unchanged, relapse on return. Correct statement: **changing jobs' efficacy depends on which exposure parameters changed, not on the new badge**. |
| 4 | "One long vacation will repair chronic burnout" | **CONTRADICTED (as a repair claim)** | Vacations have short-term positive effects on health and well-being, but **multiple metas consistently report effects decaying to near baseline within weeks of returning to work** (FACT-grade anchors: de Bloom 2009 PMID 19096200; J Appl Psychol 2025 PMID 39836131 · decay speed is MODEL-grade); the chronic burnout's exposure source untouched → relapse on return. The vacation's positioning = interval + restart window, not treatment. |
| 5 | "Energizing supplements / energy drinks / adaptogen miracle pills can treat burnout" | **CONTRADICTED (no evidence) + risk note** | No drug or supplement is approved for a burnout indication (extrapolated from ICD-11's non-disease definition + regulatory fact · INFERENCE); "anti-fatigue" supplements lack RCTs on burnout outcomes (UNKNOWN: no positive RCT found as of this round's search — not proof of ineffectiveness, but the burden of proof sits with the claimant); caffeine/stimulants worsen sleep destruction → entering the §2.3 vicious chain (MODEL · wired to mb:B1). **Stimulating through exhaustion = turning off the smoke alarm while the fire keeps burning.** |

**This canon's own falsifier**: ① any PMID anchor retracted/corrected → the corresponding section downgraded; ② WHO/ILO revises the long-hours risk framing → §2.1 rewritten; ③ a registered drug with a burnout indication appears → §3.1's medication row voided; ④ a same-criterion burnout meta on a China-representative sample appears → §1.4's UNKNOWN closed.

## 6. Unknowns and limitations (UNKNOWN as first-class citizen)

1. **Burnout prevalence for China's full-time workers**: no single-criterion meta retrieved (§1.4 · gap).
2. **Long-term (>12-month) maintenance of individual-face interventions**: decay direction consistent; the precise decay function not locked (§3.3).
3. **Night-shift individual risk function**: 2A is hazard identification; "N years of night shifts → individual probability" has no available formula (§2.2).
4. **Burnout → hard endpoints' independent effect**: burnout's own independent contribution to cardiovascular events (controlling for depression) is weaker-evidenced than the "long hours / job strain" exposure face — this canon therefore presses its hard evidence on **exposure** rather than on **scale scores** (a design choice · stated honestly).
5. Currency of legal texts: §4.1 cites institutional text; for labor disputes defer to the currently effective version + professional legal opinion.
