# Family First Aid — English L1 (2026-09-13)

> **Emergency number in this volume = 120 (mainland China's medical emergency line)** — readers elsewhere: use your local emergency number (112 / 911 / 999). Source's international coverage: UNKNOWN. *(translator calibration)* Legal, direction-level: PRC Civil Code Art. 184 ("Good Samaritan clause" (好人条款)): voluntary rescuers bear no civil liability for harm to the person helped (FACT).

## ① Principles

**Scene safety first (FACT)** — never create a second casualty: cut power; ventilate gas, no sparks; drowning rescue stays ashore. **Don't move the injured (MODEL)**; spine-suspected: one-axis move only, else wait. **Calling 120 can be the whole job (MODEL)** — dispatchers coach CPR and bleeding control; report location/event/casualties; **they hang up first**. **Check response** (tap shoulders/shout; infant soles): no response + no normal breathing (or only gasping) = arrest → CPR (FACT · AHA 2025).

## ② CPR (心肺复苏)

Untrained → **Hands-Only**; compressions alone are never worse (FACT · AHA). Adults (FACT · AHA 2025 Part 7): lower half sternum (胸骨); **5–6 cm, 100–120/min** ("Stayin' Alive"); full recoil; trained 30:2. Children/infants (FACT · MSD): depth = ⅓ chest (~4–5 cm; >55 kg teens = adult); infant two fingers; breaths 30:2 (infant pairs 15:2). **AED (自动体外除颤器) five steps (FACT)**: on → pads (right collarbone + left nipple line; pediatric for children) → clear → shock if advised → **compressions immediately** (re-analysis every 2 min). It won't shock someone who doesn't need it — the danger is not using it. Stop: response/breathing back (recovery position (侧卧位)), AED prompt, professionals arrive, exhaustion. **Red line: 100% of people who received CPR go to hospital.**

## ③ Choking — 2025 "5 back blows + 5 thrusts"

Can cough or speak = partial: **don't strike or thrust** — encourage coughing, stay close (FACT·relayed). Silent, hands at throat, cyanosis = severe → act; never blind-sweep a conscious mouth. Adults/children >1 y (FACT·relayed: AHA/ILCOR 2025): 5 back blows between shoulder blades, head low; 5 abdominal thrusts (海姆立克法) — fist two fingers above navel, inward-and-upward; alternate until it exits. Collapse → 120 + CPR. Infants <1 y: **no abdominal thrusts** — alternate 5 face-down back blows + 5 chest thrusts; remove only visible objects. Pregnancy/obesity: chest thrusts; alone: chair back. **Red line: any abdominal thrust → medical check (organ injury, FACT·relayed); collapse/CPR → hospital.**

## ④ Stroke — BEFAST; never feed

**B**alance · **E**yes · **F**ace · **A**rm · **S**peech · **T**ime — call 120, **record the exact onset time** (FACT). Thrombolysis ≤4.5 h; thrombectomy to 24 h (selected cases). **Give no water, food, or pills (FACT)**: swallowing may be paralyzed; aspirin before CT can worsen hemorrhage. **Red line: no "wait and see" — all go now, even resolved symptoms (TIA can progress).**

## ⑤ Burns — Cool–Remove–Soak–Cover–Send (冲·脱·泡·盖·送)

Cool running water ~15–20°C for **15–30 min** (helps within 3 h; FACT). Remove clothing while cooling; **never burst blisters (水泡)**; cover loosely; **nothing on the burn** — toothpaste/soy/oil/flour contaminate (FACT · ANZCOR 9.1.3); send. Never ice a burn (frostbite on burn). Chemicals: brush dry powder, flush ≥30 min; **water-reactive substances (sodium/potassium; quicklime (生石灰) brushed off first) never get water**; eyes: flush open 15–20 min. **Red lines (all go)**: face/hands/feet/perineum/joints/genitals; infants and elderly; bigger than the patient's palm; any chemical/electrical; inhalation signs (hoarseness, singed nasal hair).

## ⑥ Bleeding & trauma

**Direct pressure first (FACT · ANZCOR)**: press hard; soak-through = add layers, never peel; elevate. **Tourniquet (止血带) = last resort (FACT)**: only pressure-uncontrollable limb bleeding — ischemia, necrosis, reperfusion injury; 5–8 cm above, off joints, **write the start time**, never cover → hospital always. Fractures (MODEL): splint across both neighboring joints; never repush bone. Nosebleed (FACT): lean **forward**, pinch soft nose 10–15 min; head-back swallows blood. **Red lines**: pressure 20–30 min fails; clear watery nasal drip after trauma (CSF leak); anticoagulated; recurrent.

## ⑦ Poisoning & carbon monoxide

**No induced vomiting (催吐) with corrosives** (second burn; never "neutralize"), **hydrocarbons** (aspiration pneumonia), **or drowsiness/convulsions (FACT)**; otherwise don't self-induce — call 120/poison control, **bring the container**, skip folk antidotes. **CO (一氧化碳)**: hold breath, enter low, ventilate, cut source, no sparks; move out; drowsy = recovery position; no breathing = CPR; moderate/severe → **hyperbaric-oxygen (高压氧舱)** hospital. **Red line: all poisonings get evaluation; CO even "awake" risks delayed brain injury.**

## ⑧ Falsified "common sense"

**philtrum pressure (掐人中)** to wake anyone — delays CPR (FACT). Toothpaste/soy/oil on burns — traps heat (FACT). Head-back nosebleeds — swallows blood (FACT). Upside-down drowning "water draining" — delays oxygen; trained = 5 breaths first (FACT·relayed). Fish-bone rice/vinegar — drives bone deeper (FACT); cough gently or ENT (耳鼻喉科). Seizure mouth-objects/limb-pinning — teeth, obstruction, fractures; tongue-swallowing is myth (FACT); **time it**, >5 min or repeated → emergency. Ice on burns — frostbite (FACT; see ⑤). "Coma = stroke, pinch awake" — hypoglycemia to hemorrhage (MODEL): breathing → side + 120; none → CPR.

## Red-line master table

| Scenario | Must go to hospital | Leave to professionals |
|---|---|---|
| CPR/AED | 100% | never stop mid-resuscitation without instruction |
| Choking | post-thrust check; any collapse | infants/pregnancy/obesity first |
| Stroke | all, with onset time | lysis/thrombectomy in-hospital only |
| Burns | face/hand/foot/perineum/joint, large-area, young/old, chemical/electric/inhalation | water-reactive chemicals on scene |
| Bleeding | tourniquet users; >20–30 min pressure; anticoagulated | spine-suspected: don't move |
| Poisoning/CO | all; CO moderate/severe → hyperbaric center | corrosives/water-reactive: no gastric emptying |
