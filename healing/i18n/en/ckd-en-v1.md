# Chronic Kidney Disease Canon v1 (English · CKD deep face — a seven-face close reading of the silent organ)

> **In one sentence** (master line): **Chronic kidney disease is the disease you can test for but cannot feel — not feeling it is its nature; being able to test for it is its way out. The gap between one-in-ten awareness and one-in-ten prevalence is the seam this canon exists to close.**

**Division of labor with the urology canon**: the urology-kidney canon (uro-canon) covers the urinary family overview and conceptual foundations ("kidney deficiency" ≠ kidney disease, stones, prostate, infection, blood in urine); this volume does the deep specialty face of chronic kidney disease (CKD) alone. The two volumes interlink without repeating: concept clarifications, the low-back-pain falsification, and the supplement-harm history are rooted in the urology canon; this volume cites rather than restates.

## Face ① Epidemiology: the silence of 120 million people

### 1.1 China: 10.8% and 119.5 million (FACT · anchor-by-anchor verified)

- **Zhang L et al., Lancet 2012 national cross-sectional survey** (PMID 22386035 · abstract text verified live via TinyFish→EPMC this round): a nationally representative sample invited 50,550 people, 47,204 participated (about 47 thousand). CKD definition = **eGFR<60 ml/min/1.73m² or albuminuria** (either one counts — this definition itself plants the seed for Face ②). The survey found an adult CKD prevalence in China of **10.8%** (95%CI 10.2–11.3), projecting to about **119.5 million** adults nationwide [FACT: sample size, definition, and article identity verified via EPMC abstract; the two numbers 10.8%/119.5M match the task framing and the urology-kidney receipt, with the truncated-abstract portions not re-verified word-for-word — held at FACT×0.9 confidence].
- **The "latest-verified" framing**: the **sixth national survey of chronic disease and risk factors**, published 2023 (Wang L et al., JAMA Intern Med 2023; PMID 36804760 · EPMC verified), re-estimated China's CKD burden with updated data. Directional conclusion (INFERENCE): the prevalence burden remains high, higher in older groups, albuminuria more common than severe filtration loss — the numeric details belong to the original article [UNKNOWN: this round did not verify that paper's percentages word-for-word, and does not pretend to].
- In one sentence: **about 1 in 10 Chinese adults has CKD, and about 9 of those 10 don't know it.**

### 1.2 Awareness of only about ten percent (MODEL)

- Long-term awareness of their own disease among Chinese CKD patients has hovered around **10%** [MODEL: task framing consistent with the urology-kidney receipt; this round's EPMC did not verify the percentage word-for-word — the epistemic state is not upgraded to FACT].
- This is not "a special Chinese constitution"; it is CKD's **pathological structure** (see 1.3) multiplied by the **screening gap**: the kidney's compensatory reserve is huge + the only early window is on a lab slip — if you never test, you never know. The US framing shows the same disease, same structure: CDC estimates about 87% of people with CKD don't know it, and about 1/3 with severe CKD still don't (interlink uro:ckd-silent-90pct).

### 1.3 Global: death ranking keeps climbing (FACT×MODEL)

- **GBD 2021** (Global Burden of Disease Study 2021) CKD topic update "Global, regional, and national burden of chronic kidney disease, 1990–2021" (PMID 42456776 · EPMC verified to exist on-topic) shows global CKD burden rising continuously across 1990–2021 [FACT: article identity verified]. CKD has entered the front rank of global causes of death and kept moving up over three decades, and is listed by GBD among the priority non-communicable diseases [MODEL: the exact rank and percentages were not verified word-for-word — no invented numbers].
- The drivers of the rise (INFERENCE · transparent derivation): rising diabetes and hypertension prevalence (the denominators the two big causes feed CKD keep growing, see Face ③) + aging + a growing pool of acute kidney injury survivors + rising detection. The elderly-comorbidity polypharmacy chain interlinks with the aging canon (agc:polypharmacy_harm).

### 1.4 Why "silent" is structural, not neglect (MODEL)

- The kidneys filter about 180 liters of filtrate a day (physiology textbook framing, interlink uro:kidney-filter-organ); the compensatory reserve is enormous: remove one kidney and the other carries most of the load. **Only when eGFR has fallen below about half does serum creatinine clearly climb out of the reference interval** — dictated by the mathematical shape of the creatinine–GFR curve (falsification item in Face ④).
- So "no symptoms" ≠ "no disease," and "checkup normal" ≠ "kidneys normal" (depends on what was checked — was urine ACR done? interlink chk:normal-checkup-safe). Silence is not a reason to manufacture anxiety; it is the reason to **aim screening resources at high-risk groups** (Face ② section 2.4).

## Face ② Staging and screening: coordinates on a heat map, and a window earlier than creatinine

### 2.1 eGFR five stages (G1–G5) + albuminuria three stages (A1–A3) (FACT · guideline framing)

KDIGO 2024 CKD guideline (verified this round via its interpretive literature: PMID 40197825 pharmacist-facing interpretation, PMID 39556063 KDOQI US commentary — both EPMC-verified) continues the **CGA three-axis staging**: Cause + GFR grade (G) + albuminuria grade (A):

| G stage | eGFR (ml/min/1.73m²) | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| G1 | ≥90 | Normal or high filtration (counts as CKD only with kidney-damage markers) |
| G2 | 60–89 | Mildly decreased (same condition) |
| G3a | 45–59 | Mild-to-moderately decreased |
| G3b | 30–44 | Moderate-to-severely decreased |
| G4 | 15–29 | Severely decreased |
| G5 | <15 | Kidney failure (the range where dialysis/transplant is considered) |

| A stage | Urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio ACR (mg/g) | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | <30 | Normal to mildly increased |
| A2 | 30–300 | Moderately increased (formerly "microalbuminuria") |
| A3 | >300 | Severely increased |

- **CKD definition**: abnormality of kidney structure or function **for >3 months** (eGFR<60 or markers such as albuminuria) — the word "chronic" has a explicit time gate; a one-off abnormal lab result is not CKD (interlink chk:trend-over-point: trend over point).
- KDIGO 2024 update points (FACT · verified via interpretive literature): the eGFR estimating equation updated (2021 race-free equation); **cystatin C** combined with creatinine for confirmatory eGFR; emphasis on comprehensive management under CGA positioning. The heat map (green-yellow-orange-red) crosses the G×A axes into progression-risk color blocks — **the same eGFR number with a different ACR means a completely different risk**.

### 2.2 Why urine ACR is earlier than creatinine alone (FACT)

- Zhang 2012's CKD definition (eGFR<60 **or** albuminuria) is itself the evidence: a large share of CKD patients are defined into the cohort **while creatinine/eGFR has not yet fallen and albuminuria is already positive** [FACT: definition verified from the abstract; the "earlier" mechanism is that albuminuria reflects glomerular filtration-barrier injury, preceding overall filtration decompensation — FACT-level pathological consensus].
- Practice face: urine ACR is a ratio measurable from a **single urine sample** (albumin corrects for urine-concentration interference), cheap, non-invasive, addable to a routine checkup; testing only "kidney function (creatinine)" misses the entire A2/A3 early band. **Early detection matters for real**: albuminuria is itself a bidirectional predictor of progression and cardiovascular death (MODEL · guideline-consensus framing).

### 2.3 The falsification of "normal creatinine = normal kidneys" comes in Face ④ (coordinates set here)

eGFR and creatinine are in a nonlinear inverse relationship (Face ④ 4.4 gives the full proof). This section only sets the conclusion: **the screening window = two legs, eGFR + urine ACR; lose one and you limp.**

### 2.4 Who should screen proactively (MODEL · guideline framing)

High-risk list (any one item → periodic eGFR + urine ACR, usually annually, frequency set by nephrology by stage):

1. **Diabetes** (interlink diab:kidney_screening — the diab canon has already established the annual urine albumin/creatinine rhythm);
2. **Hypertension** (interlink cardio:htn_silent_killer, cardio:htn_home_protocol — blood pressure and kidney wear on each other bidirectionally);
3. **Cardiovascular disease** (the heart-kidney axis: heart failure, coronary disease, stroke history);
4. **Family history of CKD** (including polycystic kidney families — see Face ③ 3.4);
5. **Long-term users of kidney-toxic drugs**: long-term painkillers (NSAIDs, see Face ⑤ 5.3), long-term herbal remedies/supplements of unknown composition (interlink uro:supplement-real-harm, liver:herbal-dili — liver and kidney on one chain);
6. Others: obesity, advanced age, prior acute kidney injury, autoimmune disease, recurrent urinary obstruction/stones (interlink the uro canon).

Baseline for everyone: routine checkup urinalysis + serum creatinine is the floor; with any high-risk item above, **adding urine ACR** completes the window (interlink the checkup canon).

## Face ③ The two big causes and three special corners: diabetes, hypertension, and IgA/polycystic/drug-induced

### 3.1 Diabetic kidney disease (DKD) — cause number one (MODEL · interlink diab canon)

- Globally and in China, **diabetes is the leading cause of CKD and of "kidney failure entering dialysis"** (guideline/epidemiology-textbook framing, MODEL). About **1/3 of people with diabetes will develop evidence of kidney disease** (DKD) [MODEL: textbook-level approximation; interlink diab:progression_rate and diab:kidney_screening].
- Mechanism in one sentence (MODEL): chronic high glucose soaks the glomerular microvessels (the organ-level version of the diab canon's "chronic soaking" master line) → basement-membrane thickening, mesangial expansion, hyperfiltration → albuminuria → fibrosis. **DKD's biggest window is the decade-plus when there is only albuminuria and creatinine is still normal** — which is why the diab canon sets annual urine-albumin testing as a rhythm.
- Interlink: SGLT2i kidney-protection evidence is detailed in this volume's Face ④ (the evidence covers diabetic and non-diabetic CKD alike; diab canon diab:sglt2_glp1 interlink).

### 3.2 Hypertensive kidney damage — cause number two, two-way attrition (MODEL · interlink cardio canon)

- Hypertension → glomerular hypertension → arteriolar sclerosis and ischemic sclerosis (benign nephrosclerosis) → falling filtration rate; falling filtration rate → sodium/water retention and renal ischemia signals → still higher blood pressure. **The kidney is both hypertension's victim and its amplifier** [MODEL].
- Interlink cardio:htn_home_protocol: home blood-pressure self-measurement is the only monitoring end of this chain an ordinary person can close autonomously. Hitting the blood-pressure target (Face ④) is simultaneously heart protection and kidney protection.

### 3.3 IgA nephropathy — the glomerular disease commonest in China (MODEL)

- IgA nephropathy is **the commonest primary glomerulonephritis worldwide, especially frequent in East Asian/Chinese populations** (textbook framing, MODEL; diagnosis requires kidney biopsy). Within days of an upper-respiratory infection there may be visible hematuria or persistent microscopic hematuria + proteinuria; most common in the twenties to forties.
- Risk-communication point (INFERENCE): IgA nephropathy is not a disease of "inflammation, take anti-inflammatories"; its course is counted in decades, with treatment goals of pressing proteinuria down, controlling blood pressure, slowing progression — **the right response to visible hematuria is urinalysis + kidney function + urine ACR, not waiting for "internal heat" to self-resolve** (interlink the attribution-risk structure of uro:shanghuo-uti-refuted). The red-flag differential of painless visible hematuria interlinks uro:redflag-painless-hematuria.

### 3.4 Polycystic kidney disease — the genetic face (FACT · disease-entity level)

- Autosomal dominant polycystic kidney disease (ADPKD) is the commonest inherited kidney disease: multiple cysts in both kidneys progressively enlarging, compressing normal tissue, usually reaching G3 and beyond between ages 30–50; with clear **vertical family transmission** (one parent carries it, each child about a 50% chance) [FACT: disease-entity-level textbook fact].
- Action implication (INFERENCE): a confirmed polycystic kidney case in the family → first-degree relatives are worth one kidney ultrasound + blood-pressure monitoring; this is the typical scenario of item 4 of Face ②'s high-risk list.

### 3.5 Drug-induced kidney injury — the most preventable class (FACT · interlink uro/liver canons)

- NSAID long-term nephrotoxicity (mechanism and evidence interlink uro:nsaid-nephrotoxicity: dismantling the prostaglandin-dependent medullary reserve vasodilation); elderly multi-drug stacking interlinks agc:polypharmacy_harm.
- **Aristolochic acid nephropathy (AAN)**: herbs containing aristolochic acid (historical cases: Mu Tong / Fang Ji variants) causing rapidly progressive interstitial fibrosis — the Belgian weight-loss clinic case and the Taiwan large-cohort causal confirmation are classic harm evidence; the full evidence chain was established in the urology-kidney canon (interlink uro:aristolochic-aan, not restated here). This round's EPMC verification confirms AAN mechanism research (tubular-cell apoptosis/fibrosis pathways) remains an active frontier in 2025–2026 (PMID 42011760 et al.) [FACT: article-existence verification] — the "historical case" is not old news; it is a mechanism ledger still being written.
- The master defense line against drug-induced injury in one sentence (INFERENCE): **"kidney-protecting folk remedies" of unknown composition are precisely a major supply side of kidney injury** — Face ⑤ and the Face ⑦ falsification items close this loop.

## Face ④ The progression defense line: actionable leverage, from blood pressure to new drugs

### 4.1 Master principle: CKD is not "waiting for dialysis," it is a river with gates (MODEL)

CKD's progression speed is set by modifiable variables: blood pressure, albuminuria, blood glucose, smoking, weight, nephrotoxic exposures. **Proteinuria × hypertension are the two great accelerators**; press them down and the natural course before G3 can often be slowed by a scale of years-to-decades (MODEL · guideline-consensus framing). That is what "staging can change destiny" means.

### 4.2 Blood-pressure targets and the positioning of RAAS blockers (FACT×MODEL)

- KDIGO 2024 (verified via PMID 40197825 interpretive literature): **RAAS blockers (ACEI/ARB) + blood-pressure control + proteinuria management** are among the core trio of CKD management [FACT: recommendation direction verified via interpretive literature].
- The special position of RAAS blockers (MODEL): they are not ordinary antihypertensives — beyond lowering pressure they **specifically lower glomerular internal pressure and reduce proteinuria**, so CKD patients with proteinuria are often prescribed them even when blood pressure is not high (indications and dosing belong to the physician).
- Cautions (MODEL · honest boundary): after starting or up-titrating ACEI/ARB, serum creatinine may rise slightly (hemodynamic, usually acceptable) and serum potassium can rise — recheck as directed; **bilateral renal artery stenosis and pregnancy are contraindication directions**. Abruptly stopping on one's own accelerates progression instead.

### 4.3 SGLT2i: new cross-indication kidney-protection evidence (FACT · this round's key verification)

- **KDIGO 2024 explicitly recommends SGLT2i for CKD with proteinuria, with or without diabetes** [FACT: verified sentence-by-sentence via PMID 40197825 interpretive literature: "recommends sodium-glucose cotransporter 2 inhibitor (SGLT2i) therapy for kidney disease with proteinuria, with or without diabetes"] — an evidence-grade leap from "glucose-lowering drug" to "cross-indication kidney-protective drug."
- **EMPA-KIDNEY long-term follow-up** (EMPA-KIDNEY Collaborative Group, NEJM 2025; PMID 39453837 · EPMC verified): empagliflozin's long-term effect and safety in the CKD population continue (NEJM 2025;392:777-787) [FACT: article identity/journal volume-pages verified via EPMC; effect-size details not verified word-for-word · no invented numbers quoted].
- The same-class evidence chain: DAPA-CKD (dapagliflozin, NEJM 2020) and CREDENCE (canagliflozin, diabetic kidney disease) form the three-trial pillar of SGLT2i kidney protection [MODEL: precise-title searches for the two trials missed the main literature in two rounds — per discipline no PMID filled from memory; epistemic state conservatively MODEL; trial names and conclusion directions are multi-source-consistent framing].
- Evidence faces upgraded by KDIGO 2024 at the same time (FACT · same-source verification): **finerenone for type 2 diabetes with CKD**, GLP-1 receptor agonists' kidney-protective effect, statins lowering atherosclerotic risk, and nephrotoxin stewardship to prevent acute kidney injury.
- One sentence (INFERENCE): **this is the biggest increment in the CKD drug face in twenty years — "CKD with proteinuria, diabetic or not, gains a class of guideline-legitimized kidney-protective drugs."** Prescribing decisions belong to the physician's face; this volume's only job is to deliver the fact "this class exists" to the people who need to know.

### 4.4 Falsifying "normal creatinine = normal kidneys" (FACT · with falsifier)

- **Proposition**: "My checkup creatinine is normal, so my kidneys are fine."
- **Falsification one (curve shape · FACT)**: serum creatinine and eGFR are nonlinearly inversely related — creatinine only clearly climbs out of the reference interval once eGFR has fallen below about 60. That is, **creatinine within the reference range has no discriminative power for G2, even part of G3a**; a large share of Zhang 2012's CKD population was "creatinine still normal, albuminuria positive" (Face ② 2.2). Interlink uro:myth-creatinine-normal (the urology-kidney canon established the same falsification).
- **Falsification two (the elderly muscle-mass interference · FACT×INFERENCE)**: creatinine is produced by muscle metabolism. Older people / low body weight / sarcopenia produce less creatinine, and **the illusion of already-reduced kidney function with creatinine still "normal" is more severe** — the same serum creatinine value reflects a completely different eGFR at different muscle masses. Interlink agc:muscle_currency (muscle mass is the currency of aging health). KDIGO 2024's introduction of cystatin C exists precisely for this (FACT · see 2.1).
- **falsifier (the counter-evidence test of this proposition)**: if "normal creatinine = normal kidneys" were true, then albuminuria-positive people should all have abnormal creatinine — Zhang 2012's definition and data are the direct counterexample; case series of sarcopenic elderly CKD with normal creatinine are the second. Conclusion: **the criterion of "kidneys normal" is dual-normal eGFR + urine ACR sustained over 3 months, not a single creatinine column sitting inside parentheses.**

### 4.5 Three complication lines: anemia, bone-mineral, acidosis (MODEL)

- **Renal anemia**: the kidney secretes erythropoietin; after G3 secretion falls short → normocytic normochromic anemia (fatigue and reduced exercise tolerance, often misattributed to "just aging"). Treatable (ESA/iron, physician's face).
- **CKD bone-mineral disorder (CKD-MBD)**: reduced ability to excrete phosphate and activate vitamin D → phosphate retention, low calcium, secondary hyperparathyroidism → renal osteodystrophy and vascular calcification. Defense line: phosphate restriction (phosphate additives in processed foods are the big share), phosphate binders/active vitamin D as directed.
- **Metabolic acidosis**: reduced acid excretion → chronic acid load → accelerated muscle breakdown and bone dissolution (interlink the aging canon's muscle face); bicarbonate supplementation is one of the routine options from G3b onward.
- The three lines' common point (INFERENCE): all are **"silent but testable and treatable"** — which is exactly the reason regular nephrology follow-up starts at G3.

## Face ⑤ The living face: protein, salt, painkillers, and "like nourishes like"

### 5.1 Two misconceptions about protein: not "don't eat," not "eat freely" (MODEL · interlink uro canon)

- **Misconception one, "kidney disease means no protein"**: over-restriction → malnutrition → sarcopenia → (Face ④) falsely low creatinine masking the disease + reduced immunity; malnutrition in CKD is itself an independent negative prognostic factor [MODEL].
- **Misconception two, "being vegetarian makes it safe" or "high-protein fitness meals, no problem"**: in diagnosed CKD, long-term high protein (>1.3 g/kg/d magnitude) increases glomerular hyperfiltration load [MODEL · guideline-direction framing].
- **The correct reading (MODEL)**: general CKD (G1–G3 · non-dialysis) leans toward **about 0.8 g/kg/d, dominated by high-quality protein (eggs/milk/lean meat/fish/soy)**; under KDOQI framing late-stage non-dialysis CKD has lower individualized targets (uro:low-protein-boundary cites the 0.55–0.6 g/kg/d magnitude); **dialysis patients conversely need more protein**. Interlink the liver canon's protein face (the hepatic-encephalopathy ceiling and the kidney-disease floor are two different directions — do not mix them). **Individualized numbers must be tailored by nephrology + dietitian; this volume sets direction, not prescriptions.**

### 5.2 Salt: the shared lever of kidney and blood pressure (MODEL · interlink cardio canon)

- High salt → higher blood pressure + worse proteinuria + RAAS resistance — salt restriction in CKD is kidney protection and heart protection at once [MODEL]. The WHO 5g/day framing interlinks cardio:salt_5g_who; CKD patients should aim low rather than high and watch hidden sodium (processed foods, dried noodles, sauces). Low-sodium salt (potassium substitution) carries a **hyperkalemia risk face for CKD and must be evaluated by a doctor first** (INFERENCE · honest boundary; do not copy the cardio canon's healthy-population recommendation).

### 5.3 NSAIDs and the "back pain, take a painkiller" chain (FACT · interlink uro/jnt)

- Mechanism and evidence interlink uro:nsaid-nephrotoxicity (dismantling the prostaglandin-dependent medullary reserve vasodilation → acute kidney injury, and long-term cumulative exposure correlates with CKD progression).
- **Unpacking the life chain (INFERENCE)**: "back pain → self-prescribed painkiller → (if the pain is actually kidney-sourced) the painkiller keeps hurting the kidney → (if the pain is musculoskeletal) the painkiller merely delays proper care." Wrong at both ends. The mainstream cause of back pain is musculoskeletal (interlink the jnt joint/pain canon and uro:myth-lumbago-kidney); **first establish whose pain the back is carrying, then decide whether a drug is needed and which**; long-term painkiller users are themselves Face ②'s high-risk screening population.

### 5.4 "Like nourishes like" — eating kidneys to fortify the kidney (folk · traditional · independently labeled)

- "Like nourishes like / eating kidneys to fortify the kidney" is **folk and traditional analogical thinking** [folk · traditional]: mapping food shape onto organ function by analogy.
- The modern nutrition face (FACT/MODEL): animal kidney is **high-purine, high-cholesterol, high-phosphorus** organ meat; for CKD patients, phosphorus and purines are precisely two of the groups that need limiting (CKD-MBD + uric acid). High-temperature grilling of kidney adds a heterocyclic-amine burden.
- Layered conclusion (layering, not judgment): understandable as a traditional food-culture concept; **as a kidney-protective measure for CKD patients it is unevidenced and counterproductive (high phosphorus, high purine), and as a "kidney-fortifying" attribution it delays proper screening**. Isomorphic to "bone broth builds calcium" (falsified in the agc canon): **the nutritional ledger of organ soups does not appreciate with cultural meaning.**

### 5.5 The real harm face of "kidney-protecting" supplements (FACT · interlink uro/liver canons)

- The three-bill ledger of "kidney-fortifying" supplements interlinks uro:supplement-real-harm: aristolochic acid (uro:aristolochic-aan), illegal adulterants (sibutramine/bumetanide class), heavy metals. The herbal-DILI chain interlinks liver:herbal-dili, liver:natural-safe-falsifier (the "natural = safe" falsification).
- The CKD-specific amplifier (INFERENCE): CKD patients' renal clearance and excretion are already reduced, so **the same unknown-composition herb or supplement has a larger marginal kidney harm**; and supplement users are often precisely the people who "know their kidneys are bad" — the supply side precisely targets the anxious. **Those who never test rely on luck; those who take supplements recklessly have sold even their luck at a marked price.**

## Face ⑥ Dialysis and transplant preview: laying out the "last map" makes it less frightening, not more

> Position statement: this face is a **preview, not a sentence** — for readers at G1–G3 the only useful information here is "knowing there is a fallback + knowing why intervention should start now." All concrete end-stage-kidney decisions belong to the nephrologist's face.

### 6.1 Two dialysis modes: survival and quality of life, stated as they are (MODEL)

- **Hemodialysis (HD)**: usually 2–3 sessions a week at a dialysis center, about 4 hours each, plus vascular access (see 6.3) and the center rhythm; **peritoneal dialysis (PD)**: uses one's own peritoneum, daily exchanges at home, high autonomy, suited to work/commute/long-distance lives.
- Survival and quality of life (MODEL · honest framing): in most observational cohorts and registry data, HD and PD have **broadly comparable overall survival** (a direction of PD being not inferior in the young, without major comorbidity); the choice is driven mainly by **lifestyle, family support, residual kidney function, cardiovascular status**; the two modes can be switched sequentially — it is not "one wrong choice forever." Specific trade-offs and numbers belong to the nephrology visit [UNKNOWN: this round did not verify RCT-grade survival differences word-for-word, no invented numbers].
- The honest boundary (INFERENCE): dialysis is maintenance treatment, not a cure — it replaces filtration, not the full endocrine function; but it is **not "waiting for death while alive"**: years to over a decade of survival with substantial quality of life under proper dialysis is the realistic face; most fear comes from imagination, not data.

### 6.2 Kidney transplant: the realistic face (MODEL)

- Transplant is the option with the best quality of life and long-term survival in ESRD treatment [MODEL · consensus framing]; the Chinese real-world constraint = **uncertain donor waiting time**, often counted in years, with dialysis maintenance usually continuing through the wait.
- Three waiting realities (INFERENCE): ① the earlier the registration, the better-positioned the wait (stability on dialysis is also one premise of transplant eligibility); ② matching and immune risk are managed by the hospital, and lifelong immunosuppression follows surgery — "transplant = cured" is a misreading (interlink the "once and for all" psychology of cardio:myth_stent_cure_all); ③ the legal/ethical framework of living donation — consult a transplant center for specifics.

### 6.3 Early fistula creation: leaving time for maturation (FACT · guideline framing)

- Hemodialysis needs reliable vascular access; the **autogenous arteriovenous fistula (AVF)** is the preferred access direction ("fistula first" framing; individual exceptions assessed by the physician). AVF surgery itself is small, but **requires a maturation period of about 2–3 months** (venous arterialization and thickening) before it can tolerate needle use [MODEL: the maturation period is a KDOQI vascular-access guideline approximation].
- Action implication (INFERENCE): at G4 (eGFR<30) one should already discuss access planning with nephrology — **the cost of "waiting until dialysis is near to create the fistula" is a temporary catheter (high infection and stenosis risk)**. Early fistula creation is the most typical "trade calendar for complications avoided" leverage point in this canon.
- Pre-embedded actions protecting future access veins (MODEL): avoid repeated wrist-vein blood draws / indwelling needles consuming the cephalic vein where possible — the future fistula grows on exactly those veins.

## Face ⑦ Falsification face (≥6 items · each with falsifier)

> Format: proposition → falsification → falsifier (what evidence would test this item). Epistemic states labeled item by item.

### 7.1 "Normal creatinine = kidneys are fine" (CONTRADICTED → falsified)

See Face ④ 4.4 for the full proof. Falsifier: the counterexample of an albuminuria-positive, creatinine-normal population (Zhang 2012's definition structure) + the creatinine-normal illusion in sarcopenic elderly CKD. **The dual criterion (eGFR+ACR) sustained over 3 months is the judgment** [FACT].

### 7.2 "Back pain = bad kidneys" (CONTRADICTED → falsified)

- **Falsification**: the great majority of back pain is musculoskeletal (lumbar strain/disc/facet joints; interlink the jnt joint-pain canon, uro:myth-lumbago-kidney). Renal colic has a characteristic face (unilateral waxing-waning colic radiating to the groin, patient pacing restlessly; interlink uro:renal-colic), a different face from sitting-related soreness. And the attribution chain "back pain = kidney deficiency = fortify the kidney" is miswired at its second link already (uro:shenxu-not-nephropathy).
- **The reverse risk**: genuinely kidney-sourced back pain (pyelonephritis: back pain + fever = emergency; interlink uro:redflag-pyelo) gets delayed by "lumbar strain" self-diagnosis. Harm at both ends.
- Falsifier: in pain-clinic etiology data for back pain, the kidney-sourced share stays in single digits long-term [MODEL · direction]; the characteristic face of renal colic is textbook-level [FACT · disease-entity level].

### 7.3 "Once on dialysis, never off" (CONTRADICTED → falsified · the boundary is acute/chronic)

- **Falsification**: this myth compresses two populations into one. For **acute kidney injury (AKI)**, dialysis is mostly a temporary bridge (days to weeks; the kidney recovers and dialysis is exited — after surviving AKI, most kidney function recovers or partially recovers [MODEL · direction]); for **chronic kidney failure (sustained G5)**, starting dialysis is usually lifelong maintenance, because it is replacement after irreversible loss of kidney function — not dialysis "addiction." The causal chain is "the kidney failed, so dialysis," not "dialysis, so the kidney failed."
- Falsifier: case series of kidney-function recovery and dialysis exit after AKI dialysis exist and are common [MODEL · direction]; any data zeroing the recovery rate would contradict the clinical reality of AKI bridge dialysis.
- A subsidiary proposition also falsified: "dialysis = fading fast" → see 6.1's survival face, stated honestly.

### 7.4 "Kidney disease forbids exercise" (CONTRADICTED → falsified)

- **Falsification**: no guideline position forbids exercise in kidney disease; on the contrary, regular moderate-intensity exercise is a consensus component of CKD management (benefiting all four lines: blood pressure/glucose/weight/sarcopenia) [MODEL · guideline-direction framing]. G1–G3 without other contraindications follow general-population exercise advice; G4–G5 and dialysis patients also have exercise-rehabilitation prescriptions (individualized by nephrology/rehabilitation).
- Falsifier: population data that "exercise accelerates CKD progression" do not exist (directional evidence points to better prognosis in exercisers) [INFERENCE · to be corrected if high-quality counter-evidence appears]. What genuinely needs avoiding is a trio of add-ons, not exercise itself: sudden over-training triggering rhabdomyolysis (myoglobin striking the tubules), painkiller abuse around exercise, and unknown-composition supplements — **the danger is in the stacking, not in the exercise.**

### 7.5 "Supplements/herbs protect the kidney" (CONTRADICTED → falsified · with a documented harm face)

- **Falsification**: no supplement holds an evidence-based position for CKD protection (FACT · regulatory and evidence faces), while the documented harm face stands — aristolochic acid nephropathy (the Mu Tong / Fang Ji historical cases, full chain interlink uro:aristolochic-aan) confirmed from population exposure through mechanism research (this round's EPMC verification: the 2025–2026 AAN mechanism frontier, PMID 42011760 et al. · FACT); the illegal-adulterant and heavy-metal faces interlink uro:supplement-real-harm; the "natural = safe" master falsification interlinks liver:natural-safe-falsifier.
- **The real kidney-protection checklist** (a memory contrast point): control pressure, control glucose, quit tobacco, limit salt, moderate protein, caution with NSAIDs, screen on the high-risk rhythm, take RAASi/SGLT2i as directed when indicated — all free or insurance-covered, zero "IQ tax."
- Falsifier: producing positive RCT hard-endpoint evidence (eGFR slope/ESRD incidence) in a CKD population for any single supplement would overturn this item; no such evidence exists [UNKNOWN → this item stands].

### 7.6 "Proteinuria turned negative = cured" (CONTRADICTED → falsified)

- **Falsification**: proteinuria turning negative (especially under treatment) is **remission** — a strong signal of greatly reduced progression risk — but the pathological substrate (the metabolic memory of diabetic kidney disease, the immune-dysregulation diathesis of IgA nephropathy) mostly remains, and relapse on stopping or over time is common. CKD's "chronic" definition is precisely >3 months of structural/functional abnormality; remission ≠ structural restoration [MODEL].
- Falsifier: relapse-rate data after remission (spontaneous or post-withdrawal; IgA nephropathy/membranous nephropathy relapse common is textbook framing) [MODEL · direction]. The correct action: after turning negative, recheck on the nephrology rhythm (interlink chk:trend-over-point) — not filing the lab slip into a drawer.

### 7.7 "Drink more water to flush the kidneys / detox" (CONTRADICTED → falsified · staged and domain-scoped)

- **Falsification**: healthy kidneys filter 180L a day; "drink more, filter more" has no linear amplification (interlink the physiology ledger of uro:water-boundary); pouring in liters over hours is instead a hyponatremia risk (uro:myth-water-overload).
- **Staged, domain-scoped (INFERENCE)**: ① most early CKD: drink adequately in portions until urine is pale yellow — no "flushing therapy"; ② **dialysis/oliguric/heart-failure patients: reverse fluid restriction** (typically 1.5–2L/day including soup, porridge, fruit water, set by the physician) — in this domain the same sentence "drink more water" is a doctor's-order-level error; ③ recurrent stone formers: there is real "high fluid intake prevents stones" evidence (interlink uro:water-rct-borghi1996) — but that is the stone domain, not to be extrapolated into "a universal CKD kidney-protection method."
- Falsifier: positive RCT evidence that increasing fluid intake improves eGFR slope in CKD populations would overturn this; existing fluid-intervention trials (CKD WIT et al.) point neutral [MODEL · direction · precise citation requires later anchor-by-anchor verification].
