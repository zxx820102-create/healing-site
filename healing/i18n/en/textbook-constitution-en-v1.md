# The Constitution Textbook v1 — Nine Constitutions, the National-Standard Self-Test, Dietary Guidance, and the Evidence Boundary

> **Readers and how to use this book**: self-learners and health workers, read systematically from Chapter 1; if you only want to act right away, Chapter 0 alone is enough. This book's unique anchor — **a national standard exists for constitution classification (GB/T 46939-2025)**: if you want to test, use the national-standard questionnaire, not the slapdash tests spread by self-media. This book also carries the strictest discipline: the nine-type classification itself belongs to the [TCM·Traditional] system (internal logic of a traditional system; efficacy unproven), and **having a national standard does not mean having modern efficacy validation** — the two layers are never mixed anywhere in this book. Every conclusion is tagged with a confidence tier (the four plain-language tiers), and the section-end anchors [L3:…] lead back to the evidence-layer canon wording.

## Chapter 0. One-page action version (L1)

> The full one-pager lives at `docs/healing/textbook/constitution-l1-80yi-20260912.md` and can be distributed on its own; key points below.

**In one sentence**: constitution is a description of tendencies, not a diagnosis; the national standard's 27 items are the proper self-test tool, and the result is a clue, not a label.

**Seven action cards**: ① if you want to test your constitution, use the national standard's 27 items (about 4 minutes) and skip the slapdash self-media tests — the result is a clue, not a diagnosis (confirmed by research: the standard has been published and is in force); ② most people are composite constitutions — don't pin a single name-tag on yourself (fairly well established); ③ don't glue on permanent labels — tendencies can change with diet and daily habits (fairly well established); ④ tending overweight, heavy body, sticky mouth (phlegm-dampness / damp-heat tendency): control weight, limit alcohol, cut rich-sweet foods and late-night meals — the strongest face where tradition and modern evidence overlap; ⑤ cold intolerance with cold hands and feet (yang-deficiency tendency): warming foods of the jujube-ginger-lamb-soup kind, not "yang-tonifying drugs" — with marked cold intolerance, see a doctor first (traditional dietary-nourishment face); ⑥ low mood with chest tightness (qi-stagnation tendency): rose flower / albizia flower as a tea substitute — and for mood problems, see a doctor when seeing a doctor is due (traditional dietary-nourishment face); ⑦ allergic constitution (special type): strictly avoid allergens once confirmed, plus nutritional substitution; when unconfirmed, don't broadly avoid "fawu" trigger foods (confirmed by research).

**Myth quick-check**: constitution is fixed from birth (no — it can be adjusted) ｜ damp-heat needs a violent "detox" (no — dietary nourishment is not a detox course) ｜ yang deficiency relies on "yang-tonifying drugs" (no — check the cause first) ｜ nine constitutions = nine kinds of people (composite types are the most common) ｜ having a national standard = efficacy validated (no — it is an integration of observational studies) ｜ self-test = being ill (no — just a clue).

**Not known**: whether yang-deficiency ≈ low thyroid function (direct evidence vacant); whether eating by constitution improves outcomes (no controlled-trial-level evidence exists); whether the special/allergic type corresponds to modern allergy markers (no high-signal evidence obtained).

## Chapter 1. What constitution is: from traditional classification to a national standard

**Learning objectives**: be able to state clearly the difference between the two layers — "the standard exists = confirmed" and "the standard's content = a traditional system"; be able to retell the national standard's three-stage history and its three major upgrades.

### 1.1 Definition and the two-layer discipline

Constitution in the Chinese-medicine sense means a relatively stable combination of tendencies in a person's body structure, physiological function, and psychological traits — it describes "which way you tend to lean," and does not answer "what illness you have right now." This book's core discipline: the nine-constitution classification is a [TCM·Traditional] system; **"an industry standard / national standard exists" is a verifiable normative fact (confirmed by research), but the classification's content and its illness-tendency claims do not automatically gain modern-evidence status just because a national standard exists** — the national standard's "evidence-based update" refers to the constitution-research community's integration of observational studies, not controlled-trial-level validation. The two layers must not be mixed. [L3:A 总纲]

### 1.2 Three stages of the lineage (all verified)

Table 1-1. Three stages of the lineage:

| Stage | Facts | Tier |
|---|---|---|
| Classification takes shape (2005) | Wang Qi's nine-type classification paper published in the Journal of Beijing University of Chinese Medicine, funded by the national 973 Program; the nine types = balanced / qi-deficiency / yang-deficiency / yin-deficiency / phlegm-dampness / damp-heat / blood-stasis / qi-stagnation / special-allergic; the intellectual lineage traces back to the Huangdi Neijing (Inner Canon) and other traditional writings | Paper exists = confirmed by research; content = [TCM·Traditional] [L3:A1] |
| Industry standard (2009) | The China Association of Chinese Medicine's Classification and Determination of Constitution in TCM, 60-item version; incorporated the same year into the national basic public health service specification; by the end of 2022, grassroots application had exceeded 570 million person-times, covering "zhi wei bing" departments of more than 1,700 secondary-level-or-above TCM hospitals (official accounting) | Scale of application = confirmed by research (official accounting); validity of the determination = internal reliability/validity of the system, not clinical-outcome validation [L3:A1] |
| National standard (2025) | GB/T 46939-2025, a voluntary national standard: issued by the State Administration for Market Regulation, drafting organized by the National Administration of Traditional Chinese Medicine, led by Wang Qi with Beijing University of Chinese Medicine coordinating more than 20 participating institutions; in force from 2026-04-01 | Standard history = confirmed by research [L3:A1] |

"Zhi wei bing" = the traditional preventive concept of regulating and protecting the body before illness arises; it is not a validated technology.

### 1.3 The national standard's three major upgrades (per the launch briefing)

① Based on research from 2009–2024 and about 400,000 cases of epidemiological survey, the characteristic descriptions and illness tendencies of the nine constitutions were updated — **qi-stagnation gained "depression" and "cognitive frailty," damp-heat gained "hyperuricemia"** (the additions are an integration of observational evidence, not new causal evidence); ② the Constitution in Chinese Medicine Questionnaire (CCMQ) was cut from 60 items to **27 items**, with completion time down from 11.6 minutes to **4.23 minutes**; ③ the industry group standard was elevated to a national standard (history = confirmed by research). [L3:A1]

## Chapter 3. How to use the suited-avoid lists: where tradition and modern evidence overlap and diverge

**Learning objectives**: point out the three places where the traditional suited-avoid lists overlap modern nutrition science and the two places they diverge; explain why the overlapping places can be followed with confidence and the diverging places must stand side by side.

### 3.1 The overlapping face (stated honestly — the traditional sayings and modern evidence landed in the same direction)

① **Phlegm-dampness × weight control**: phlegm-dampness avoids rich-sweet-thick-greasy foods, alcohol, and late-night eating; the modern face points the same way — sugar-sweetened beverages promoting obesity is confirmed-by-research level, and the phlegm-dampness association with obesity/prediabetes has reached association-aggregation level (fairly well established). [L3:C5/B] ② **Damp-heat × gout's alcohol-and-meat avoidance**: damp-heat avoids spicy food, alcohol, and fried food; on the modern face, gout's avoidance of high-purine / alcohol / fructose is likewise confirmed-by-research level — the two behavioral recommendations converge, the most direct China–West interface in this book; note that the new national standard writing "hyperuricemia" into the damp-heat tendency is a standard update (observational integration), not new causal evidence. [L3:C6] ③ **Yin-deficiency × don't eat scalding hot**: tradition says scalding food "damages the jinye body fluids"; modern science says beverages and foods above 65 °C increase esophageal-cancer risk (confirmed by research · International Agency for Research on Cancer Group 2A) — "scalding damages the body fluids" and "scalding damages the mucosa" arrive at the same destination by different routes. [L3:C4] In these three places, follow the modern face with confidence; the traditional sayings simply wrote the same recipe earlier.

### 3.2 The diverging face (kept honestly side by side — not merged, not adjudicated)

① **Yang-deficiency × iced drinks**: tradition lists iced drinks as a major taboo for yang deficiency (the cold-congealing logic); modern science has not confirmed that iced drinks reduce core heat production — treat it as an individual self-trial item (if it feels bad, drink less), not a universal ban. [L3:C3] ② **Special/allergic × fawu trigger foods**: tradition avoids "fawu" (shrimp, crab, goose, mutton, alcohol, spicy); the modern discipline is **strict avoidance only for confirmed allergens** — broad avoidance without confirmation is a nutritional risk, children especially. The three layers are carried verbatim, not merged into a single "don't eat any of it." [L3:C9]

### 3.3 General principles of use

The suited-avoid lists are **dietary-nourishment references** (recipe level), not prescriptions and not treatment; for people taking Western pharmaceuticals, drug–food conflicts take priority over constitution-based dietary nourishment. [L3:A2]

**Summary**: the three overlaps can be followed with confidence (weight control, gout's alcohol-and-meat avoidance, no scalding-hot food — confirmed on the modern face); the two divergences stand side by side (iced drinks as a self-trial, fawu pending confirmation); no suited-avoid list replaces treatment.

## Chapter 4. The honest face of modern research: association exists, causation does not

**Learning objectives**: retell the ceiling of the modern evidence and the four honest boundaries; recognize "constitution science has been validated"-style sales talk.

### 4.1 State of the evidence (as of the 2026-09 search face)

All of the modern research is cross-sectional / cohort / bioinformatic exploration — evidence at the level of "statistical association exists between constitution × metabolic markers / genes," with no causation and no intervention validation. The strongest cell is **phlegm-dampness**: association aggregation in obese populations (phlegm-dampness is over-represented among obese adults in China) and association aggregation in prediabetes populations (the constitution distribution is non-uniform) — two studies reaching the **fairly well established (association-aggregation)** level, but still observational aggregation, with the mechanism part being network pharmacology (algorithms predicting component–target relations), not lab measurement. [L3:B] The remaining anchors — phlegm-dampness/damp-heat × hyperuricemia, qi-deficiency × aerobic capacity (mediated via the altitude-sickness face), yin/yang-deficiency × sleep quality, yin-deficiency × aging phenotypes, yin-yang deficiency × transcriptomic differences, yang-deficiency × tumor microenvironment, blood-stasis × inflammation index × stroke stratification, qi-stagnation × depression polygenic risk (Taiwan Biobank n=11,030) — are all at the **still being studied (preliminary signal)** level. [L3:B]

### 4.2 Four honest boundaries

① Within the searched face there are **no** controlled trials of "eating by constitution → outcome improvement" (only 1 AI constitution-education trial at protocol stage) — whether "constitution-based eating works" is not known; ② cross-sectional designs cannot rule out reverse causation (falling ill → constitution score changes); ③ the study populations are heavily concentrated in China; ④ effect sizes are generally small to moderate. [L3:B]

### 4.3 The framework itself is also an object of testing

In a validity study of the same questionnaire in a Japanese population, a five-type model fit better — the nine-type structure has **population relativity** (fairly well established). The typing is a tool, not nine compartments in nature. [L3:B]

**Summary**: what the modern face supports is only that "stratified exploration is worth doing"; to date the constitution dimension is a hypothesis generator, not an evidence-based prescribing rule. [L3:D 总裁决]

## Chapter 6. Red lines: four kinds of real harm

**Learning objectives**: retell the four red lines and identify which one you are most likely to step on.

① **Self-test misjudgment**: a 27-item self-rating scale has limited specificity; composite constitutions are common; the typing has population relativity — a self-test result = a clue, not a diagnosis; cross-validate through a proper constitution-identification clinic or modern examinations. [L3:E1] ② **Delayed medical care**: constitution-based dietary nourishment does not replace treatment; a damp-heat "hyperuricemia tendency" ≠ not checking and not lowering uric acid; a special/allergic tendency ≠ skipping allergen testing; with clear symptoms, see a doctor first (the symptom-avoidance matrix red lines apply without exemption). [L3:E2] ③ **Constitution type fixation**: constitution is an adjustable state-tendency, not a fate label (traditional theory itself presumes acquired changeability — daoyin intervention studies take "constitution is movable" as their premise); gluing on a dead label → one-sided food avoidance → a narrowed nutritional face → self-limitation; **no constitution-based food avoidance for children or pregnancy**. [L3:E3] ④ **Commercial capture**: once the national standard rolls out, "constitution testing + customized dietary nourishment" easily becomes marketing talk — for anyone selling supplements or treatment courses in the name of constitution, first ask the evidence level, first check drug–food conflicts. [L3:E4]

## Chapter 9. Frequently asked questions (FAQ · 11 questions)

1. **A national standard exists — doesn't that mean the constitution theory has been scientifically validated?** No. The national standard is the standardization of a classification-and-determination method (history = confirmed by research); the "evidence-based update" is an integration of observational studies, not validation by controlled trials of "eating by constitution → outcome improvement." [L3:A1/B]
2. **Where can I test properly?** Look for the national-standard 27-item version (about 4 minutes); the proper channel is a constitution-identification clinic at a medical institution; self-media quizzes are not the national standard and have no determination rules. [L3:A1/E1]
3. **The test says I am a composite constitution — now what?** That is the most common result. Just take the top 1-2 scoring types as reference; don't chase a "pure pedigree title." [L3:E1]
4. **Can constitution change?** Yes. Traditional theory itself presumes acquired changeability; when diet and daily habits change, the tendencies change, and you can re-evaluate after 6 weeks. [L3:E3]
5. **Should a phlegm-dampness type take "phlegm-resolving medicine"?** No. The suited-avoid lists are dietary-nourishment references; the modern phlegm-dampness associations concentrate in obesity/prediabetes/hyperuricemia — the action is weight control, limiting alcohol, and less rich-sweet food, not taking medicine. [L3:C5/E2]
6. **Does a damp-heat type need to "detox"?** No. "Detox packages" and "treatment courses" are marketing talk; the damp-heat suited-avoid entries are an everyday dietary-nourishment face; for gout, follow the modern behavioral discipline (limit alcohol, purines, fructose). [L3:C6/E4]
7. **Can a yang-deficiency type take "yang-tonifying drugs"?** No. "Yang-tonifying drugs" are not dietary nourishment; with marked cold intolerance, check modern causes such as thyroid function first — warming-yang foods (the jujube-ginger-lamb-soup kind) are only a recipe-level reference. [L3:C3/E2]
8. **Should an allergic constitution avoid fawu trigger foods?** Three layers: confirmed allergens → strict avoidance plus nutritional substitution (confirmed by research); traditional fawu → a traditional-face reference; unconfirmed → broad avoidance is a nutritional risk, children especially. 10. **What is the Japanese five-type model about?** In a validity study of the same questionnaire in a Japanese population, the five-type model fit better — the nine-type structure has population relativity; the typing is a tool, not a law of nature (fairly well established). [L3:B]
11. **Are "constitution testing + customized dietary nourishment" services worth buying?** Be skeptical by default: for supplements or treatment courses sold in the name of constitution, first ask the evidence level (Chapter 4) and first check drug–food conflicts; the national standard is a public-health tool, not a consumption gateway.
