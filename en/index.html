Translator calibration & annotation standard

These volumes are transcriptions, not authorship: every PMID, number, conclusion and falsifier is carried over verbatim from the Chinese source canon; no medical content has been added. Epistemic labels (FACT / FACT·relayed / MODEL / INFERENCE / CONTRADICTED / UNKNOWN) are preserved as-is — UNKNOWN is left empty rather than invented. Medical terms carry the Chinese original in parentheses at first occurrence.

Emergency numbers differ by region. Emergency numbers appearing in the volumes (e.g., 120) refer to mainland China's lines; readers elsewhere should call their local emergency number (e.g., 112 / 911 / 999). This is marked as translator calibration.

Annotation standard: state tokens, terminology and conventions follow the EN i18n Glossary v1; later volumes conform to it, and deviations are recorded as errata against it, never silently improvised.
